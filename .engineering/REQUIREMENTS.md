# NexLabs Company Requirements

Status: `BASELINE_DRAFT_FOR_WO_002`

## Company requirements

- **REQ-001 — Founder authority:** one human founder retains final authority over strategy and explicitly high-risk actions.
- **REQ-002 — AI-native workforce:** operational roles may be filled by AI agents with explicit role contracts, tools, permissions, memory boundaries, KPIs and escalation.
- **REQ-003 — Governed engineering:** software delivery must reproduce the GEF lifecycle with Work Orders, Context Locks, evidence, PR review and checkpoint promotion.
- **REQ-004 — One admitted increment:** future work may be planned, but execution requires explicit Work Order admission.
- **REQ-005 — Canonical Git truth:** approved company and engineering truth must be versioned in Git and bound to exact evidence.
- **REQ-006 — Local-first:** the initial Company OS must be developable and testable locally, using Docker where runtime services are needed.
- **REQ-007 — Provider independence:** agent/runtime/model integrations must use replaceable adapters.
- **REQ-008 — Hermes adapter:** Hermes may be the first agent runtime integration but must not become an irreplaceable company dependency.
- **REQ-009 — Executor independence:** Codex may be an executor adapter; the company architecture must not require Codex for every task.
- **REQ-010 — Autonomous engineering cell:** v0.1 must prove an end-to-end governed engineering cell from approved idea to audited increment.
- **REQ-011 — Auditability:** material agent/company actions require attributable audit records and evidence.
- **REQ-012 — Authority controls:** every action class must resolve to AUTO, AUTO+AUDIT, REVIEW REQUIRED, CEO APPROVAL, or PROHIBITED.
- **REQ-013 — High assurance:** money, Web3 signing, privileged authentication, critical security and irreversible actions require stronger proof and explicit founder approval.
- **REQ-014 — Founder visibility:** the founder must have a consolidated view of projects, agents, work, approvals, failures and company state.
- **REQ-015 — Environment portability:** local, staging and production must share portable application/runtime contracts.
- **REQ-016 — Secret safety:** secrets and credentials must never be committed to source or evidence.
- **REQ-017 — Observability:** runtime health, failures, cost/usage and agent execution must be observable.
- **REQ-018 — Portfolio governance:** product ideas must pass governed intake, validation, prioritization, build and continuation/kill decisions.
- **REQ-019 — Business operations:** financial, commercial, marketing, research and operational models must be explicitly planned before automation.
- **REQ-020 — Public/company assets:** website, portfolio, investor materials and brand must derive from approved company strategy rather than invent it during implementation.
