# NexLabs Company Scope

## Version target

`NexLabs Company v0.1`

## NECESSARY for v0.1

1. Canonical Source Pack and governance baseline.
2. Company identity and strategic boundaries.
3. Business/revenue architecture.
4. Corporate/AI authority and risk model.
5. AI workforce architecture and employee contract.
6. Product Factory and governed engineering operating model.
7. Security baseline covering AI, SaaS, Web3 and financial-risk domains.
8. Finance and commercial operating models sufficient to govern the company.
9. Company OS functional architecture and local-first runtime architecture.
10. Provider-independent agent runtime design with Hermes as first adapter.
11. Local autonomous engineering-cell MVP.
12. Integrated v0.1 acceptance against Definition of Done.

## IMPORTANT but not automatically release-blocking unless promoted by a later decision

- Research/IP system.
- Brand, website and public portfolio implementation.
- Investor-ready package.
- Founder Command Center beyond the minimum operational visibility required for the engineering-cell MVP.
- Staging/production migration beyond validated design/readiness.

## FUTURE

- Full autonomous finance operations.
- Full autonomous sales/marketing organization.
- Legal execution without human professionals.
- Unbounded self-expansion of agents.
- Fully autonomous movement of money or crypto.
- Multi-founder/equity administration.
- Large-scale multi-region infrastructure.

## OUT OF SCOPE

- Agents independently signing legal agreements.
- Agents independently moving company funds or crypto.
- Weakening GEF/security gates for speed.
- Treating conversational memory as company authority.
- Premature implementation before the governing planning Work Order is approved.
