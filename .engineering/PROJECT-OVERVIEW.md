# NexLabs Company — Project Overview

## Purpose

This repository is the canonical company-and-engineering source of truth for **NexLabs Technology**, a one-founder, AI-native technology startup whose operational workforce is designed to be composed primarily of governed AI agents.

The founder remains the ultimate authority for company direction and high-risk decisions. AI employees execute bounded roles under explicit permissions, evidence requirements, auditability and escalation rules.

## Company domains

NexLabs may research, build, operate or commercialize technology across:
- Web2 and SaaS;
- Web3, blockchain and cryptocurrency infrastructure;
- AI and automation;
- financial and market analytics;
- developer tools and infrastructure;
- games and interactive technology;
- data products and APIs;
- research and proprietary technology.

Individual products remain separately governed repositories when appropriate. This repository governs the company, its operating model, Company OS and cross-company systems.

## Operating thesis

The target model is:

`Founder intent → governed planning → Work Order → execution → tests/evidence → PR → audit → correction/approval → checkpoint → next`

This is the same evidence-first GEF workflow already used by the founder, progressively encoded into the company itself.

## Delivery strategy

The system is local-first. Company OS will be designed to run in Docker for development and validation before any mandatory VPS/cloud dependency. Runtime/provider integrations must be replaceable.
