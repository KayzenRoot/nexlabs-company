# NexLabs Technology — Product Intake

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_008`

## Minimum intake record

Every idea entering formal portfolio consideration should provide:

- `idea_id`
- `working_name`
- `submitted_by`
- `date`
- `target_user`
- `problem_statement`
- `proposed_value`
- `why_now`
- `strategic_domain`
- `primary_revenue_hypothesis`
- `distribution_hypothesis`
- `technical_hypothesis`
- `known_risks`
- `reusable_capability_potential`
- `initial_evidence`
- `assumptions`

## Intake quality

A good intake is short enough to create cheaply but specific enough to research.

Bad:
“Create an AI app for restaurants.”

Better:
“Independent delivery-focused restaurants lose margin and time updating menus/prices across channels; test whether a lightweight menu/pricing automation product can save recurring operational work and justify a low recurring subscription.”

## Assumption labeling

Separate:
- `FACT / OBSERVED`
- `EVIDENCE`
- `ASSUMPTION`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `UNKNOWN`

Do not upgrade an assumption to evidence because multiple agents repeated it.

## Rejection at intake

An idea may be rejected immediately when:
- it conflicts with company boundaries;
- the problem is incoherent;
- legal/security risk is unacceptable at current capability;
- it duplicates an existing opportunity without a differentiated hypothesis;
- it has no plausible user/value relationship;
- pursuing it would violate portfolio capacity.

## Deferred ideas

Deferred ideas should remain in an opportunity backlog with:
- reason;
- revisit trigger;
- last evidence date.

Defer is not build approval.
