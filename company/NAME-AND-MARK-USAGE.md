# NexLabs Technology — Name & Mark Usage

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_014`

## Naming hierarchy

### Corporate name
`NexLabs Technology`

Use for:
- canonical company documents;
- legal/corporate references when appropriate;
- metadata requiring the company name;
- investor/company strategy materials.

### Short company name
`NexLabs`

Use when context is already clear.

### Visual wordmark
`NEX LABS`

This is an approved typographic/visual lockup. It does not rename the company to a separate entity.

### Extended lockup
`NEX LABS / TECHNOLOGY`

Use where the full technology-company identity benefits from visual hierarchy.

## Precision Blades N

The selected N:
- must retain a strong capital-N silhouette;
- preserves the defining diagonal bridge;
- remains recognizable without glow;
- should work in monochrome;
- should remain legible at favicon/app-icon scale through governed simplification.

A generic N replacement is not acceptable simply because it is easier to implement.

## Clear-space intent

Use protected space around the symbol/lockup. The implementation repository owns exact production measurements as long as it preserves the approved geometry and legibility.

## Small sizes

At very small sizes:
- use symbol only;
- simplify internal cuts if necessary;
- preserve outer silhouette/diagonal bridge;
- do not depend on gradients, transparency or chrome for recognition.

## Misuse

Do not:
- stretch/skew the mark;
- recolor it into unrelated rainbow palettes;
- add uncontrolled drop shadows/glows;
- place it on unreadable backgrounds;
- animate continuous spin;
- rebuild it from emoji/icon fonts;
- use a third-party N logo as substitute.

## Product brands

A NexLabs product may have its own name/identity, but use of “by NexLabs” or the corporate mark must follow portfolio/public-proof rules and must not imply the product has a status it does not have.

## Legal/trademark note

These rules govern internal brand usage. They are not trademark-clearance or registration evidence.
