# NexLabs Technology — Management Financial Reporting

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_012`

## Founder finance view

A compact Founder view should eventually show:

### Liquidity
- available cash;
- restricted/reserve cash;
- runway base/downside;
- next major commitments.

### Revenue
- revenue by type/product;
- MRR/ARR where applicable;
- collection status;
- churn/retention indicators.

### Cost
- gross/net burn;
- AI/model;
- cloud;
- data/API;
- software;
- GTM;
- legal/security;
- other material cost centers.

### Unit economics
- gross margin;
- contribution margin;
- CAC/payback/LTV where credible;
- cost per successful automated task.

### Budget
- plan vs actual;
- committed;
- forecast;
- material variances.

### Portfolio
- product spend;
- product revenue;
- strategic/internal platform spend;
- active WIP cost.

### Funding
- funding scenario;
- use-of-funds status;
- milestones/tranches;
- next funding decision window.

## Reporting metadata

Every report should state:
- period;
- data freshness;
- reconciliation status;
- source;
- assumptions;
- confidence;
- currency/reporting convention.

## Exceptions

Surface exceptions rather than burying them:
- provider cost spike;
- missed collection;
- budget overrun;
- runway deterioration;
- low-margin growth;
- milestone miss;
- unusual financial execution.

## Founder attention

The goal is not to make the Founder operate a spreadsheet all day.

The system should elevate decisions, anomalies and runway risks while preserving drill-down evidence.
