# NexLabs Technology — ICP & Segmentation

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## ICP principle

An ICP is an evidence-backed description of who is most likely to obtain and recognize product value under sustainable economics.

It is product-specific.

## ICP fields

- `segment_id`
- `product`
- `user_or_account_type`
- `role/persona`
- `problem`
- `trigger/context`
- `current_alternative`
- `desired_outcome`
- `frequency/severity`
- `willingness_to_pay_evidence`
- `ability_to_pay/context`
- `buying_process`
- `technical_constraints`
- `security/compliance_constraints`
- `disqualifiers`
- `evidence_refs`
- `confidence`

## Segment confidence

- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `PARTIALLY_VALIDATED`
- `VALIDATED_FOR_CURRENT_STAGE`
- `DISCONFIRMED`

## Disqualifiers

Examples:
- problem too weak/infrequent;
- no viable budget/economics;
- unsupported environment;
- unacceptable regulatory risk;
- support burden too high;
- custom-work expectation conflicts with product strategy;
- poor retention/value pattern.

## Segment comparison

Evaluate:
- problem intensity;
- distribution access;
- willingness to pay;
- sales cycle;
- support burden;
- CAC potential;
- retention potential;
- strategic fit;
- regulation/security;
- reusable learning.

## No demographic theater

Do not invent unnecessary demographic persona details that do not affect product or buying behavior.

## Feedback

Won/lost/churn data updates segment confidence over time.
