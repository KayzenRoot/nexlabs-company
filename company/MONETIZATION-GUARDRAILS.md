# NexLabs Technology — Monetization Guardrails

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_004`

## Trust

Monetization must not depend on confusing, coercing or trapping users.

Avoid:
- hidden recurring charges;
- deceptive cancellation friction;
- surprise overage;
- fake scarcity;
- misleading ROI/performance claims;
- paywalls that contradict promised entitlement.

## Data

User/customer data is not automatically a monetizable asset.

Data monetization requires:
- lawful basis;
- clear product rationale;
- privacy/security governance;
- contractual/consent compatibility where required;
- minimization and appropriate aggregation/anonymization where applicable.

Selling personal or confidential customer data is not a default NexLabs business model.

## Advertising

Advertising may be considered for products where it fits user expectations and economics. It must not be inserted merely because traffic exists.

Ads should not:
- compromise product integrity;
- create undisclosed conflicts;
- misuse sensitive data;
- materially degrade a paid product experience without explicit product decision.

## AI costs

Products with material model/compute costs must protect unit economics through one or more of:
- included limits;
- metering;
- model routing;
- caching/reuse;
- fair-use policy;
- tiering;
- enterprise capacity commitments.

Unlimited usage is never assumed to be economically safe.

## Web3 / crypto

Permitted product revenue may include software subscriptions, infrastructure/API fees, transparent platform fees or licensing.

Not default company revenue:
- proprietary speculative trading;
- yield chasing with customer/company treasury;
- hidden token allocation economics;
- custody or movement of customer assets without the required legal/security controls.

Token issuance, custody, financial promotion, exchange-like activity or on-chain value execution requires separate legal/compliance/security authorization before operation.

## Financial analytics

Revenue may come from analytical software, data tools or subscriptions. The product must distinguish analytics/information from regulated advice or execution where applicable.

## Games

Games may monetize through:
- purchase;
- subscription;
- expansions/content;
- cosmetic/digital goods;
- season/pass structures;
- platform/marketplace economics where appropriate.

Avoid pay-to-win mechanics that undermine the intended product experience unless a specific product strategy explicitly justifies them.

## Affiliate / referral revenue

Allowed only when:
- disclosed;
- product-relevant;
- compliant;
- not allowed to distort recommendations.

## Guardrail principle

A monetization technique that increases short-term revenue while materially damaging user trust, legal posture or product retention is economically suspect, not a successful optimization.
