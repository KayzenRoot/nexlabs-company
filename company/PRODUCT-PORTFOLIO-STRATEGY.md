# NexLabs Technology — Product Portfolio Strategy

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_008`

## 1. Portfolio purpose

NexLabs operates a portfolio only when multiple products can benefit from shared company capability.

The portfolio exists to compound:
- engineering systems;
- AI workforce capability;
- research;
- distribution learning;
- reusable software;
- data/evaluation;
- operational infrastructure;
- company knowledge.

It does not exist to maximize project count.

## 2. Opportunity domains

Permitted domains include:
- AI and automation;
- SaaS / Web2;
- Web3 / blockchain / crypto infrastructure;
- financial and market intelligence;
- data products and APIs;
- developer tools;
- games / interactive technology;
- research-derived software.

A domain being permitted does not mean NexLabs must operate in it now.

## 3. Portfolio principles

### Focus before breadth
Starting a new product consumes founder attention, agent capacity, infrastructure and future maintenance.

### Strategic fit
A product should reinforce the NexLabs thesis, business model or reusable capability.

### Evidence before scale
Promising ideas may be researched cheaply. Significant build/scale requires stronger evidence.

### Reuse where real
Shared components are valuable only when they reduce cost/risk or improve quality. Premature generalization is not a portfolio advantage.

### Independent product clarity
Each product should have a clear user, problem and value proposition even if it shares internal NexLabs technology.

## 4. Portfolio buckets

### Core
Products with strong strategic importance and active company commitment.

### Growth
Validated products with evidence justifying increased resources.

### Explore
Bounded experiments/research with limited resource allocation.

### Maintain
Stable products receiving controlled maintenance rather than active expansion.

### Paused
No current investment beyond preservation/obligations.

### Retired
No longer operated except required archival/migration/support obligations.

## 5. Portfolio decision authority

AI systems may research, score and recommend.

Founder/CEO retains final authority to:
- admit a product to active build;
- change strategic portfolio priority;
- materially increase resource commitment;
- pause, pivot or retire a strategically significant product.

## 6. Portfolio health

Portfolio health is not measured by number of repos/apps.

Useful portfolio indicators eventually include:
- validated problem/value;
- retention/usage;
- revenue/economics;
- distribution efficiency;
- reliability/support burden;
- strategic learning;
- reusable capability;
- concentration/risk;
- active WIP load.

## 7. Anti-portfolio behavior

Avoid:
- cloning trends without a differentiated user/problem thesis;
- keeping dead products alive because code already exists;
- launching many products with no distribution plan;
- giving every experiment production-grade infrastructure too early;
- treating one successful metric as proof of product-market fit.
