# NexLabs Technology — Public Proof & Disclosure Policy

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_014`

## Principle

The public site may be cinematic. The facts may not be cinematic fiction.

## Proof classes

### SELF_ATTESTED
Supported by canonical internal state, e.g. mission or documented architecture.

### MEASURED
Supported by retained objective measurement/evidence.

### THIRD_PARTY_VERIFIED
Supported by external source/certification/award/publication.

### PERMISSIONED_EXTERNAL
Customer/partner/logo/quote with required permission.

## Never fabricate

- customers;
- partners;
- deployments;
- patents;
- publications;
- awards;
- certifications;
- revenue;
- funding;
- users;
- team/headcount;
- offices/geographic reach;
- performance outcomes;
- security/compliance status;
- testimonials;
- rankings.

## Implication counts as a claim

A logo wall, fake dashboard, “trusted by” section, customer-like quote or stock office map can create a false claim even when no sentence lies explicitly.

Do not use implication to bypass this policy.

## Confidentiality

Before public disclosure, check:
- personal data;
- customer/partner confidentiality;
- credentials/secrets;
- unreleased security detail;
- confidential IP;
- patent/trade-secret disposition;
- license/asset rights.

## Research

A research direction is not:
- a publication;
- a patent;
- a breakthrough;
- deployed customer capability.

Use the precise label.

## Corrections

If a public claim becomes inaccurate:
- correct/remove promptly;
- update evidence source;
- retain internal record when material.

## Founder authority

Strategically material disclosures such as funding, partnerships, major product launches and sensitive company milestones require Founder approval.
