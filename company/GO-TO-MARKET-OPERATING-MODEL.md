# NexLabs Technology — Go-to-Market Operating Model

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## Purpose

GTM converts validated product value into repeatable acquisition, conversion, adoption, retention and expansion.

It does not create fake demand for weak products.

## Canonical commercial flow

`APPROVED PRODUCT CASE → ICP → POSITIONING → CHANNEL HYPOTHESIS → DEMAND → QUALIFICATION → SALE/TRIAL → ONBOARDING → ACTIVATION → VALUE REALIZATION → RETENTION → EXPANSION / WIN-BACK / CHURN LEARNING`

## Inputs

A product enters GTM only with sufficient evidence from Product Factory:
- target problem/user;
- validated value hypothesis;
- product state;
- pricing hypothesis;
- relevant economics;
- security/regulatory constraints;
- success metrics.

## Commercial stages

### Discover
Understand market/segment/problem.

### Attract
Create demand or capture existing demand.

### Qualify
Determine whether account/user/problem actually fits.

### Convert
Move from interest to trial/purchase/contract.

### Activate
Reach first meaningful product value.

### Retain
Keep delivering recognized value.

### Expand
Increase usage/tier/scope only when value is demonstrated.

### Learn
Return won/lost/churn/support evidence to Product Factory.

## Principles

- qualified outcomes over vanity reach;
- product truth over persuasive exaggeration;
- economics over uncontrolled growth;
- lifecycle over one-time acquisition;
- evidence over attribution mythology;
- consent/privacy over lead hoarding;
- repeatable systems over heroic manual selling.

## Founder authority

Founder/CEO retains final authority over:
- company positioning;
- major pricing/packaging shifts;
- large commercial commitments;
- major channel spend;
- strategic partnerships;
- regulated/high-risk GTM.

AI commercial agents may research, qualify, draft, score and recommend within policy.
