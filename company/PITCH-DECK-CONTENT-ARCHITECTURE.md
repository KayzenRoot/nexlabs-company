# NexLabs Technology — Pitch Deck Content Architecture

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Principle

The deck is a narrative interface over canonical evidence.

It is not a separate source of truth.

## Recommended deck sequence

### 1. Cover
Company, concise category, date/version.

### 2. Problem
Specific valuable friction and who experiences it.

### 3. Market / timing
Why the opportunity exists now; evidence/method for market sizing.

### 4. NexLabs solution / company thesis
Products + AI-native company capability.

### 5. Product / portfolio
Evidence-backed current products and stage.

### 6. How the Product Factory works
Why NexLabs can turn opportunities into governed products.

### 7. Technology / AI workforce
Architecture and differentiated operating capability, without implying unproven superiority.

### 8. Business model
Revenue architecture/pricing/value capture.

### 9. GTM
ICP, channels, sales/lifecycle approach and current evidence.

### 10. Traction / proof
Only actual or explicitly labeled evidence.

### 11. Economics
Revenue/margins/CAC/LTV/runway only when credible and classified.

### 12. Competition / alternatives
Realistic alternatives and differentiation.

### 13. Team / operating model
Founder plus governed AI workforce. AI roles are not misrepresented as legal human employees.

### 14. Research / IP
Proprietary/research capabilities and actual IP status. Internal invention records are not patents.

### 15. Security / governance
Why high-risk autonomy remains bounded/auditable.

### 16. Funding ask / use of funds
Scenario/range, capital plan and runway/milestones.

### 17. Milestones
What evidence the capital is intended to unlock.

### 18. Risks / key assumptions
Material unknowns.

### 19. Closing
Why NexLabs, why now, next step.

## Optional appendix

- detailed product cases;
- architecture;
- cohorts/economics;
- market sizing;
- competitive matrix;
- milestone/tranche detail;
- data-room index.

## Market sizing

TAM/SAM/SOM or alternative sizing must identify:
- method;
- sources;
- date;
- assumptions;
- what portion is actually reachable.

Top-down industry totals without a bottom-up or ICP link are not treated as proof of opportunity.

## Competition

“ No competitors ” is not credible by default.

Include:
- direct competitors;
- indirect tools;
- manual workflows;
- internal build;
- doing nothing.

## Visual design

Deck visuals follow NexLabs brand rules but readability and evidence outrank cinematic decoration.
