# NexLabs Technology — Technology Transfer to Product

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_010`

## Purpose

Move validated research/technology into product or internal engineering without bypassing Product Factory or GEF.

## Transfer package

At minimum:
- research/innovation ID;
- problem/opportunity;
- evidence summary;
- prototype/results;
- limitations;
- environment/version;
- reusable components;
- provenance/license status;
- security/privacy concerns;
- IP disposition/status;
- cost/performance notes;
- recommended product/internal use;
- open unknowns.

## Transfer decisions

Possible destinations:

### Product Factory
When the research enables a user/product opportunity.

The product still requires normal intake/validation/portfolio decision.

### Internal platform
When value is operational/reusable across products.

Engineering still requires a governed Work Order.

### Product enhancement
When an existing product can use the technology.

Product owner/portfolio authority decides priority; GEF governs implementation.

### Licensing candidate
When the technology may have standalone B2B/IP value.

Requires provenance/business/legal review.

### Open-source/publication candidate
Requires the dedicated public-release gate.

### Archive
When no current transfer is justified.

## No automatic promotion

A prototype branch/repository is not automatically production code.

Before transfer, engineering may need to:
- rewrite;
- secure;
- test;
- benchmark;
- document;
- remove experimental dependencies;
- change licensing/dependency choices.

## Evidence continuity

Transfer should preserve links from:
`research → experiment → prototype → innovation/invention record → Product Case / Work Order → product release`

This chain allows the company to explain where differentiated technology came from.

## Founder visibility

Founder dashboard/Company OS should eventually expose:
- active research;
- prototype state;
- Innovation Ledger candidates;
- pending IP decisions;
- transfer opportunities;
- blocked provenance/legal reviews.
