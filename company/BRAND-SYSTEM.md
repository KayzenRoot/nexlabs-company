# NexLabs Technology — Brand System

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_014`

## Brand objective

NexLabs Technology presents as a premium AI-native technology company and product studio: intelligent, precise, cinematic, research-oriented, technically credible and human-centered.

The brand should feel durable enough to outlive any one model provider, product trend or visual fashion.

## Canonical identity

- Corporate name: `NexLabs Technology`
- Approved visual wordmark: `NEX LABS`
- Approved extended visual wordmark: `NEX LABS / TECHNOLOGY`
- Selected symbol direction: `NEX-N-A-PRECISION-BLADES`
- Core symbol: original capital-N abstraction built from interlocking planes/blades with a defining diagonal bridge.

Spacing in the visual lockup is a design treatment and does not create a second legal/corporate identity.

## Identity principles

### Precision
Geometry, copy and interaction should feel intentional.

### Intelligence without cliché
Do not use generic “AI brain + rainbow gradient” visual language as identity.

### Research credibility
The brand should signal experimentation, evidence and engineering discipline rather than speculative hype.

### Human-centered technology
Technology is presented as an amplifier of human understanding and capability, not as an abstract machine cult.

### Living system
Digital surfaces may feel alive through restrained energy, depth and environmental motion while remaining calm and usable.

### Factual confidence
NexLabs should look ambitious without pretending it has customers, patents, scale, awards or outcomes it cannot prove.

## Mark architecture

The flat vector silhouette is the identity source of truth.

Chrome, glass, cyan light, reflection and dimensional effects are presentation layers only. If all effects are removed, the N must remain recognizable.

Required mark families:
- symbol-only;
- horizontal `NEX LABS`;
- extended `NEX LABS / TECHNOLOGY`;
- monochrome light;
- monochrome dark;
- enhanced chrome presentation.

## Anti-patterns

Do not drift into:
- esports/gaming-clan identity;
- crypto-token visual shorthand;
- rainbow/aurora generic AI gradients;
- aggressive cyberpunk noise;
- permanent neon flooding;
- glitch as primary identity;
- over-detailed marks that collapse at small size;
- stock “futuristic technology” collage.

## Change authority

Material changes to:
- company name;
- symbol geometry;
- identity direction;
- primary visual language;
- wordmark architecture

require Founder approval and a governed change.

Implementation refinements that preserve identity may be handled in the website/design-system repository under normal GEF review.
