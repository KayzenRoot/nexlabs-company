# NexLabs Technology — Budget & Forecasting

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_012`

## Budget dimensions

Each budget line should support:
- period;
- cost/revenue category;
- product/project;
- owner;
- plan;
- actual;
- committed;
- forecast;
- variance;
- variance reason;
- source/confidence.

## Core cost categories

Examples:
- AI/model;
- cloud/compute/storage;
- data/API/provider;
- software/tools;
- payments/fees;
- security/compliance;
- legal/accounting;
- product distribution/marketing;
- contractors/human payroll when applicable;
- equipment/capex;
- insurance;
- taxes/statutory costs;
- contingency.

## Planning horizon

Maintain rolling planning rather than a once-per-year frozen spreadsheet.

Recommended views:
- next 3 months detailed;
- next 12 months operating;
- longer-term scenario-level only unless evidence supports detail.

## Variance

Variance must not disappear through silent rebasing.

Material variance should record:
- amount/%;
- cause;
- temporary vs structural;
- action;
- owner;
- forecast impact.

## Reforecast

Reforecast when:
- funding changes;
- revenue trend changes materially;
- product capacity changes;
- provider/model cost changes;
- launch slips;
- churn/retention changes;
- regulation/security creates new cost;
- major incident occurs.

## Budget admission

A budget allocation does not itself authorize payment.

Execution still follows spend/treasury authority and HIGH_ASSURANCE rules.

## Confidence

Forecast confidence may be:
- LOW;
- MEDIUM;
- HIGH.

Confidence reflects evidence quality, not optimism.
