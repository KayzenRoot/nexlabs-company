# NexLabs Technology — Investor Risk Disclosure

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Principle

Risk disclosure improves investment quality. It is not a list of boilerplate designed to scare or reassure.

## Risk categories

### Company / key-person
- founder concentration;
- early-stage operating maturity;
- hiring/capacity constraints.

### Product
- weak PMF;
- retention;
- product complexity;
- support burden;
- roadmap concentration.

### Market / competition
- market timing;
- incumbents;
- pricing pressure;
- distribution access.

### GTM
- unproven channels;
- CAC;
- sales-cycle uncertainty;
- churn.

### Financial
- burn/runway;
- fundraising dependency;
- margin/cost volatility;
- concentration.

### AI / model/provider
- model/provider changes;
- pricing;
- reliability;
- lock-in;
- quality drift;
- policy/access changes.

### Technical / infrastructure
- availability;
- scalability;
- technical debt;
- dependency/supply-chain risk.

### Security / privacy
- compromise;
- credential/key exposure;
- personal-data incidents;
- third-party risk.

### Web3 / financial-regulatory
- custody/signing;
- asset volatility;
- BCB/CVM/applicability;
- irreversible transactions.

### Legal / IP
- ownership/provenance;
- third-party licenses;
- trademark;
- patentability/FTO;
- contractual disputes.

### Execution / autonomy
- agent errors;
- insufficient controls;
- automation overreach;
- Founder attention constraints.

## Risk record

For each material risk:
- statement;
- likelihood/confidence;
- impact;
- leading indicators;
- controls;
- residual risk;
- owner;
- review trigger.

## Investor narrative

Do not hide a risk because it complicates the pitch.

Explain how it is understood and managed.

## Legal note

This internal risk framework is not a substitute for counsel-prepared securities/offering risk disclosures.
