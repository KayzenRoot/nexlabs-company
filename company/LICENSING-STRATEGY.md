# NexLabs Technology — Licensing Strategy

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_010`

## Inbound licensing

Every material third-party dependency/license should be compatible with intended use.

Review dimensions:
- commercial use;
- modification;
- redistribution;
- attribution/notice;
- copyleft/source obligations;
- patent clauses;
- trademark restrictions;
- model/data restrictions;
- hosted/SaaS implications.

## Outbound licensing

NexLabs technology may eventually be:
- proprietary/internal;
- commercially licensed;
- dual-licensed;
- open-source;
- source-available;
- bundled into a product/API.

Disposition depends on strategy, provenance, security and legal review.

## No agent signing authority

AI agents may:
- summarize license text;
- flag obligations;
- compare alternatives;
- prepare a proposed licensing package.

They may not independently:
- accept binding terms on behalf of the company where CEO approval/legal authority is required;
- sign commercial licenses;
- grant exclusive rights;
- waive company IP;
- approve high-risk reciprocal obligations.

## License register

Company OS should eventually track:
- inbound licenses;
- outbound licenses;
- affected products/assets;
- expiry/renewal;
- notices;
- restrictions;
- approval refs.

## Licensing candidate

A technology becomes a licensing candidate only after:
- ownership/provenance sufficiently understood;
- reusable value demonstrated;
- support/security implications assessed;
- business model considered;
- Founder disposition recorded.

## Legal review

Material exclusivity, indemnity, liability, patent, sublicensing, geographic/field restrictions or unusual open-source obligations should be escalated to qualified counsel.
