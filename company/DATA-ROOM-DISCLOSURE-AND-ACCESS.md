# NexLabs Technology — Data Room Disclosure & Access

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Access classes

### PUBLIC
Already safe for public release.

### INVESTOR_SHARED
Appropriate for qualified investor access.

### CONFIDENTIAL_DILIGENCE
Sensitive diligence material requiring stricter controlled access.

### LEGAL_RESTRICTED
Legal/privileged/highly sensitive records; share only under explicit Founder/legal process.

### DO_NOT_SHARE
Secrets, raw credentials, seed/private keys, unnecessary sensitive personal data or material that must not be distributed.

## Disclosure principle

Investor interest is not blanket authorization.

Share the minimum information necessary for the diligence stage.

## Access controls

Protected data room should support as appropriate:
- named user access;
- MFA;
- expiration;
- least privilege;
- audit/access logs;
- download restrictions/watermarks when useful;
- revocation.

## Personal data

Minimize/redact personal data.

Do not share:
- unnecessary IDs;
- passwords;
- private addresses;
- sensitive employee/customer details

simply because a diligence request asks broadly.

## Security details

Provide enough security evidence to assess maturity without disclosing exploitable secrets unnecessarily.

## IP

Confidential invention detail/trade secrets may require staged disclosure, NDA or counsel guidance.

## Customer/partner information

Respect contractual confidentiality and permission.

Aggregate/redact where sufficient.

## Founder approval

Material sensitive disclosure requires Founder authorization.

## Expiration

Access should be reviewed/revoked when:
- diligence ends;
- investor drops;
- information becomes stale;
- access risk changes.
