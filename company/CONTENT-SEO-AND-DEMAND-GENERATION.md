# NexLabs Technology — Content, SEO & Demand Generation

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## Principle

Content should answer real user/customer problems and create durable trust/demand.

Search traffic is a means, not the product.

## Content sources

Content may derive from:
- validated user questions;
- product use cases;
- research;
- benchmarks;
- technical insights;
- case studies with permission;
- integration/tutorial needs;
- market education.

## SEO

Prefer:
- search intent alignment;
- useful original information;
- clear information architecture;
- technical performance/accessibility;
- accurate metadata;
- internal linking;
- structured content when appropriate.

Avoid:
- keyword stuffing;
- mass low-value page generation;
- duplicate doorway pages;
- fabricated author/expertise;
- scraped/copyright-infringing content.

## AI-generated content

AI may assist drafting/research, but publication requires:
- factual review;
- source/proof check;
- brand/product consistency;
- no fabricated experience/data;
- no confidential material;
- appropriate copyright/provenance handling.

## Demand generation

A demand campaign needs:
- target ICP;
- problem;
- offer;
- CTA;
- channel;
- measurement;
- budget/cap;
- stop condition.

## Lead magnets

Lead capture must respect privacy, consent and data minimization.

Do not collect unnecessary fields because a form can.
