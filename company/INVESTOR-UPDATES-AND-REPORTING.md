# NexLabs Technology — Investor Updates & Reporting

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Purpose

Investor updates maintain trust through consistent facts, decisions and risks rather than only reporting good news.

## Suggested update structure

### 1. Executive summary
What materially changed.

### 2. Product
Releases, validation, usage/retention and blockers.

### 3. Commercial
Pipeline/conversion/customers/revenue/retention where actual.

### 4. Finance
Cash, burn, runway, budget variance and forecast changes.

### 5. Product Factory / Company capability
Material improvements in reusable/autonomous operating capability.

### 6. Milestones
Met, missed, at risk and evidence.

### 7. Risks / incidents
Material new or changed risks.

### 8. Team / workforce
Founder/human and AI-workforce capability changes, accurately described.

### 9. Decisions / asks
Introductions, hiring, partnerships, expertise or capital decisions where relevant.

### 10. Next period
Key priorities and assumptions.

## Metric consistency

Use the Investor KPI Contract.

Do not change definitions between updates without disclosure.

## Cadence

Cadence is set by financing agreement/Founder policy.

Possible internal default for active institutional investors may be monthly/quarterly depending on stage, but no cadence is canonically promised by this document.

## Bad-news rule

Material misses should be reported with:
- what happened;
- impact;
- current evidence;
- corrective action;
- changed forecast.

Do not delay disclosure solely to make the next update look better.

## Confidentiality

Investor updates are not automatically public marketing content.
