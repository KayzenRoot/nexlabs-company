# NexLabs Technology — AI Workforce Staffing Phases

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_006`

Staffing is based on operational complexity and evidence, not company vanity.

## Phase 0 — Founder + assisted engineering

Use while company foundations are being built.

Active capabilities:
- Founder/CEO;
- planning/architecture;
- repository executor;
- tests/review.

Goal:
- build canonical company sources;
- prove governed execution.

## Phase 1 — Minimum viable AI company

Trigger:
- Company OS / autonomous engineering cell begins real local operation.

Add persistent logical coordination:
- AI Chief of Staff;
- AI Product/Strategy;
- AI Technology/Engineering.

On-demand:
- Security;
- Research;
- Operations;
- Finance;
- Commercial;
- specialist engineering.

Goal:
- Founder can issue priorities instead of manually orchestrating every engineering step.

## Phase 2 — Repeatable product factory

Trigger:
- multiple product opportunities/increments are active and coordination becomes a real bottleneck.

Possible added persistent capabilities:
- Product Operations;
- Research Lead;
- Security Lead;
- Operations/Platform Lead.

Keep Finance/Commercial elastic unless workload justifies persistence.

Goal:
- repeatable product validation/build/launch loops.

## Phase 3 — Multi-product operation

Trigger:
- multiple live products create recurring operational/commercial load.

Possible persistent functions:
- COO-like coordination;
- CFO/financial operations;
- CMO/Growth;
- Customer Success;
- SRE/Platform;
- Security Operations.

Goal:
- products operate concurrently without forcing Founder into routine coordination.

## Phase 4 — Scaled AI-native company

Trigger:
- portfolio complexity, risk and revenue justify dedicated functional structures.

Possible structure:
- executive AI coordination layer;
- functional AI departments;
- product pods;
- specialist review pools;
- external human professional network where law, contracts, accounting or domain assurance require it.

Goal:
- high leverage without sacrificing authority, auditability or quality.

## Promotion criteria

A role becomes persistent when one or more are proven:
- recurring workload;
- coordination latency is materially harming throughput;
- context continuity produces measurable benefit;
- dedicated monitoring is required;
- risk/segregation requires independence;
- economics justify persistence.

A role should remain elastic when demand is intermittent.

## Deactivation

A persistent role may return to elastic/on-demand status when:
- workload falls;
- its function becomes automated deterministically;
- another role can safely absorb it;
- the product/function is retired.

Organizational complexity is reversible.
