# NexLabs Technology — Case Study Standard

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_014`

## Purpose

A case study explains verified work, decisions, evidence and outcomes with enough context to be credible.

## Case-study type

Every case study declares one:

- `INTERNAL_PRODUCT`
- `INTERNAL_PLATFORM`
- `RESEARCH_PROJECT`
- `OPEN_SOURCE_PROJECT`
- `CLIENT_PROJECT`
- `PARTNERSHIP_PROJECT`

Client/partnership types require real evidence and permission.

## Structure

1. **Context**
2. **Problem / question**
3. **Constraints**
4. **Approach**
5. **Architecture / system**
6. **Key decisions**
7. **Evidence / validation**
8. **Result / current state**
9. **Metrics**, only when verified
10. **Limitations / what remains**
11. **Reusable learning**
12. **Links/media**, when public

## Internal work

For NexLabs-owned products/projects:
- clearly say it is internal/owned;
- do not write “the client needed”;
- do not imply a paid external engagement.

## Results

A result may be:
- validated hypothesis;
- working prototype;
- production release;
- performance improvement;
- rejected approach;
- reusable internal technology.

Not every case study needs a dramatic success story.

## Metrics

Use the same measured/evidence conventions as Finance/GTM/public portfolio.

## Visuals

Screenshots, diagrams and video must:
- reflect actual artifacts or clearly labeled conceptual material;
- avoid private data/secrets;
- preserve source/provenance;
- not imply functionality not present.

## Customer quotes

Require real attributable source and permission as applicable.

AI-generated quotes are never testimonials.

## Review

Public case studies receive factual/proof review before publication.
