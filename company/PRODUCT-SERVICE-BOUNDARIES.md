# NexLabs Technology — Product / Service Boundaries

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_004`

## Default

NexLabs is a product company. Services are an instrument, not the identity.

## A service is strategically admissible when

At least one strong reason exists:
- it drives adoption of an owned NexLabs product;
- the work can become reusable product capability;
- it gives access to a strategically valuable workflow/problem;
- it funds a bounded productization experiment with explicit limits;
- it supports an enterprise product deployment without becoming bespoke core software.

And all of the following are true:
- scope is explicit;
- economics are measurable;
- manual burden is bounded;
- IP/ownership is clear;
- security/data access is governed;
- the work does not bypass portfolio priorities.

## A service should be rejected or redesigned when

- revenue depends primarily on selling founder/agent hours;
- each client needs a unique codebase;
- maintenance obligations grow linearly with customer count;
- the engagement has no reusable learning/capability;
- customer urgency repeatedly displaces owned-product roadmap;
- margins are unknowable because AI/cloud/human-support costs are unmeasured;
- the work creates material compliance/security exposure without the required governance.

## Productized services

A repeatable service can become a productized service when:
- inputs/outputs are standardized;
- process variation is bounded;
- delivery can be substantially automated;
- pricing and acceptance criteria are clear;
- support burden is predictable.

Productized services may be useful bridges toward full software products.

## Custom enterprise work

Allowed only under explicit product/contract governance. Custom work should normally live at the edge of the product through configuration, integrations or bounded extensions rather than forking the product core.

## Strategic rule

If service revenue grows faster than product capability, leadership must explicitly decide whether that is intentional. Drift into an agency model is not automatic approval.
