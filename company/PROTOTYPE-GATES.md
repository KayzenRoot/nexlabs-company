# NexLabs Technology — Prototype Gates

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_010`

## Gate P0 — Research question

Before prototype work:
- bounded technical question exists;
- success/failure criteria defined;
- cheapest useful experiment considered.

## Gate P1 — Prototype authorization

Prototype may proceed when:
- scope is bounded;
- risk/environment is acceptable;
- required data/models/code can be used legally/contractually;
- cost is acceptable.

## Gate P2 — Technical proof

Ask:
**Did the prototype demonstrate the intended technical property under the tested conditions?**

Return:
- PASS;
- PARTIAL;
- FAIL;
- INCONCLUSIVE.

## Gate P3 — Reproducibility

Where material:
- rerun;
- record environment/version;
- quantify variance;
- confirm result is not a one-off artifact.

## Gate P4 — Production relevance

Ask:
**What would have to change before this is production-capable?**

Consider:
- reliability;
- scale;
- security;
- latency;
- cost;
- maintainability;
- licensing;
- observability.

## Gate P5 — Disposition

Choose:
- stop/archive;
- research more;
- transfer to Product Factory;
- build internal platform capability;
- mark Innovation Ledger candidate;
- request legal/IP review;
- prepare open-source/publication evaluation.

## Prototype ≠ product validation

A successful prototype does not prove:
- people want it;
- users will pay;
- distribution works;
- production cost is acceptable;
- the idea is legally protectable.

Those belong to their appropriate gates.
