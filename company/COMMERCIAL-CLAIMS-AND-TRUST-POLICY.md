# NexLabs Technology — Commercial Claims & Trust Policy

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## Principle

NexLabs does not purchase short-term conversion with long-term credibility.

## Prohibited claims/practices

Do not fabricate:
- customer logos;
- testimonials;
- reviews;
- usage/customer counts;
- revenue/traction;
- awards;
- partnerships;
- certifications;
- benchmarks;
- market share;
- ROI;
- scarcity/stock/countdown urgency.

Do not present:
- forecast as actual;
- internal target as customer result;
- prototype as production capability;
- planned integration as available;
- AI-generated quote as customer testimony.

## Evidence levels

A material commercial claim should have:
- claim;
- evidence/source;
- scope/conditions;
- date/version;
- approval if high risk.

## Testimonials/case studies

Require:
- real customer/source;
- permission when needed;
- accurate context;
- no material editing that changes meaning.

## ROI claims

ROI/time-saved/performance claims need:
- measured basis;
- assumptions;
- workload/context;
- limitations.

## Scarcity

Scarcity/availability may be stated only when real.

## Security/compliance

Do not claim:
- “secure” as absolute;
- certified/compliant status not actually achieved;
- data residency/retention capabilities not implemented.

## Regulated/high-risk domains

Financial, health, legal, crypto/Web3 or other regulated claims require product-specific applicability review.

## AI agents

Agents may draft persuasive copy, but policy/evidence outrank conversion goals.

When proof is missing, say less rather than invent more.
