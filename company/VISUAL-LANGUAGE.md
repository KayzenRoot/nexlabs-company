# NexLabs Technology — Visual Language

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_014`

## Core visual character

The NexLabs visual world is:
- dark;
- architectural;
- cinematic;
- precise;
- spacious;
- technically elegant;
- alive at low amplitude.

It should resemble an advanced research/engineering environment more than a dashboard template.

## Material roles

### Primary field
Near-black / blue-black / graphite.

### Primary text
Cold white.

### Secondary text
Desaturated blue-gray.

### Primary energy
Ice cyan.

### Secondary energy
Electric blue.

### Tertiary highlight
Restrained violet.

### Enhanced material
Silver / chrome / glass.

The company authority defines these roles. Exact production color tokens may live in the implementation design system and must remain consistent with these roles.

## Composition

Prefer:
- asymmetric editorial layouts;
- strong negative space;
- one dominant visual hierarchy;
- environmental continuity;
- precise rails/frames/nodes;
- layered depth;
- restrained translucent surfaces.

Avoid filling every area.

## 3D / imagery

3D is allowed when it strengthens the story.

Preferred:
- monumental Precision Blades N;
- research/lab environments;
- global/network imagery;
- architectural layers;
- human scale cues;
- abstract infrastructure objects.

3D must not become decorative burden.

## Interface surfaces

Cards/panels:
- restrained translucency;
- strong contrast;
- subtle edge light;
- readable content hierarchy.

HUD-like detail:
- sparse;
- meaningful or purely decorative but clearly non-semantic;
- never required to understand content.

## Typography direction

- headings: geometric/grotesk presence with excellent legibility;
- body: neutral, highly readable grotesk;
- micro-labels/navigation: compact, deliberate tracking.

Production fonts require appropriate licensing and Latin coverage.

## Responsive behavior

Mobile does not shrink desktop.

It recomposes:
- semantic content first;
- dominant brand visual second;
- reduced decorative density;
- no tiny HUD;
- no horizontal scroll.

## Anti-patterns

Reject:
- generic gradient blobs;
- rainbow AI palette;
- “hacker terminal everywhere” look;
- gaming HUD overload;
- noisy neon;
- excessive glassmorphism;
- decorative complexity that harms performance or contrast.
