# NexLabs Technology — Investor Data Room Information Architecture

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Purpose

The data room lets an authorized investor/diligence party verify the company efficiently without exposing everything publicly.

## Suggested structure

### 00 — Index & Read Me
- data-room index;
- version/date;
- confidentiality/use notice;
- primary contacts.

### 01 — Company & Corporate
- entity/incorporation records;
- governance;
- ownership/cap-table authoritative records;
- material corporate decisions.

### 02 — Strategy & Business
- company overview;
- business model;
- product portfolio;
- roadmap;
- market/competition.

### 03 — Products & Technology
- product summaries;
- demos/evidence;
- architecture;
- technical roadmap;
- reliability/quality evidence.

### 04 — AI Workforce / Company OS
- operating model;
- governance;
- autonomous engineering architecture;
- relevant metrics/evidence.

### 05 — Finance
- historical financials as available;
- management reporting;
- budget/forecast;
- cash/burn/runway;
- unit economics;
- funding model.

### 06 — GTM & Commercial
- ICP;
- pipeline summaries;
- commercial metrics;
- customer evidence where permission allows;
- churn/retention.

### 07 — Security, Privacy & Compliance
- security architecture summaries;
- policies;
- incidents as appropriate;
- privacy/data governance;
- compliance applicability.

### 08 — Research & IP
- innovation ledger;
- invention disclosures;
- licenses;
- open-source posture;
- patents/applications only if real;
- IP assignments/ownership evidence where applicable.

### 09 — Legal & Contracts
- material contracts;
- terms/policies;
- disputes/claims if any;
- regulatory/legal analyses where applicable.

### 10 — People / Workforce
- Founder/team;
- contractors/employees where applicable;
- AI workforce architecture;
- employment/contractor matters as appropriate.

### 11 — Fundraise
- executive summary;
- pitch;
- ask/use-of-funds;
- milestone plan;
- financing scenarios;
- diligence Q&A.

### 12 — Risks & Insurance
- risk register;
- insurance if any;
- business continuity;
- material incidents.

## Stage-aware population

An empty section should state `UNAVAILABLE / NOT_APPLICABLE / NOT_YET_REQUIRED` rather than contain fabricated documents.

## Confidentiality

This repository stores the IA/index standard only.

Sensitive data-room files belong in a protected access-controlled system.
