# NexLabs Technology — Portfolio Scoring

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_008`

Scoring helps comparison. It does not replace Founder/CEO judgment.

## Dimensions

Suggested normalized dimensions:

### Strategic Fit
Does this reinforce NexLabs thesis, reusable capability or intended market position?

### Problem / User Value
How painful/frequent/valuable is the problem or desired outcome?

### Evidence Quality
How much is observed versus assumed?

### Market / Distribution
Can NexLabs reach users efficiently enough to learn and eventually sell?

### Technical Feasibility
Can the MVP reach required quality/reliability with available capability?

### Economic Potential
Is there a plausible sustainable value-capture model and cost structure?

### Reuse / Compounding
Will building/operating it improve reusable company capability?

### Differentiation / Defensibility
Is there a credible path beyond trivial copying?

### Risk
Security, regulatory, privacy, support, capital and operational risk.

### Founder / Strategic Conviction
A transparent explicit judgment input, not a hidden override.

## Score semantics

Use a small scale such as 0–5 only when evidence supports comparison.

Do not imply mathematical certainty.

A product with high total score may still be blocked by:
- unacceptable risk;
- missing critical evidence;
- capacity;
- founder decision;
- dependency.

## Evidence confidence

Attach confidence:
- LOW;
- MEDIUM;
- HIGH.

Example:
`Distribution = 4 / confidence LOW` is very different from `4 / HIGH`.

## Risk treatment

Risk should not merely be another positive point.

Certain risk states are gates and can block admission regardless of total score.

## Decision output

Recommended:
- reject;
- defer;
- research;
- validate;
- prototype;
- specify/build;
- maintain;
- scale.

The final portfolio decision is recorded separately.
