# NexLabs Technology — Open Source & Publication Policy

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_010`

## Principle

Public release is a strategic disposition decision, not the default endpoint of research.

## Release gate

Before open-source/publication, verify:

### Provenance
NexLabs has sufficient rights/permission to release the material.

### Licenses
Third-party obligations are compatible and required notices are included.

### Secrets
No credentials, keys, internal tokens or sensitive configuration.

### Security
No exploitable internal details that should remain private.

### Confidentiality
No customer/partner/confidential data.

### IP disposition
The material is not unresolved:
- patent-review candidate;
- trade-secret candidate;
- confidential proprietary candidate.

### Brand/product
Naming, trademarks and support expectations are clear.

### Founder approval
Strategically material release has explicit Founder authorization.

## Open-source reasons

Valid reasons may include:
- ecosystem adoption;
- developer trust;
- standardization;
- community contribution;
- recruiting/reputation;
- complementing proprietary products;
- reducing maintenance duplication.

## Reasons to keep private

May include:
- core differentiation;
- security-sensitive internals;
- uncertain provenance;
- unreleased product strategy;
- licensing opportunity;
- confidential research;
- legal/IP review pending.

## Publication

Research publication must distinguish:
- measured results;
- hypotheses;
- limitations;
- proprietary detail omitted;
- reproducibility information where safe.

Do not publish fabricated benchmarks or unsupported “state-of-the-art” claims.

## Emergency stop

If release provenance or sensitive-content status becomes uncertain, stop publication until reconciled.
