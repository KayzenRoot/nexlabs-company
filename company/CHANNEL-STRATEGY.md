# NexLabs Technology — Channel Strategy

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## Channel principle

Channels are hypotheses about repeatable access to qualified demand.

No channel is canonical for every product.

## Potential channel families

- organic/search;
- content;
- communities;
- social;
- product-led loops;
- partnerships/integrations;
- direct outbound;
- paid acquisition;
- marketplaces/app stores;
- affiliates/referrals;
- events;
- founder-led sales;
- enterprise sales.

## Channel experiment record

- channel;
- ICP/segment;
- hypothesis;
- offer/message;
- budget/cap;
- time window;
- leading metric;
- downstream success metric;
- expected economics;
- stop condition;
- result;
- learning.

## Downstream metrics

Prefer:
- qualified lead/opportunity;
- activation;
- paid conversion;
- retention;
- contribution margin;
- CAC/payback.

Traffic, reach and impressions may diagnose a channel but are not sufficient commercial success.

## Channel concentration

A successful channel may create platform dependency.

Track:
- concentration;
- policy risk;
- paid-cost inflation;
- algorithm/search changes;
- partner dependency.

## Paid acquisition

Do not scale paid spend merely because CAC looks low on immature attribution.

Confirm:
- conversion quality;
- refunds/fraud;
- retention;
- gross margin;
- cohort payback.

## Founder approval

Large channel spend or strategic partnerships require the applicable Founder approval.
