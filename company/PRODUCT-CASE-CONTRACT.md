# NexLabs Technology — Product Case Contract

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_008`

A Product Case is the minimum decision package required before meaningful build admission.

## Required sections

- `product_id`
- `working_name`
- `lifecycle_state`
- `target_user`
- `problem_statement`
- `evidence_summary`
- `assumptions_and_unknowns`
- `value_proposition`
- `primary_use_case`
- `mvp_scope`
- `non_goals`
- `primary_revenue_hypothesis`
- `distribution_hypothesis`
- `success_metrics`
- `technical_feasibility`
- `major_cost_drivers`
- `security_privacy_risk`
- `regulatory_legal_risk`
- `reusable_capability`
- `portfolio_score`
- `founder_decision`
- `decision_date`

## Build admission

A Product Case does not itself authorize arbitrary coding.

It authorizes preparation of the appropriate engineering Source Pack/Work Orders when Founder/CEO admits build.

## MVP scope

The MVP should test the core value/risk hypothesis, not simulate a complete mature company.

## Success metrics

Metrics must state what evidence would change the decision.

Examples:
- activation threshold;
- repeat use/retention;
- paid conversion;
- workflow time saved;
- quality benchmark;
- cost-to-serve;
- user feedback pattern.

Avoid vanity-only metrics.

## Assumptions

Unknown is an acceptable field value.

Do not fabricate market size, conversion, revenue or customer evidence to make the case look complete.

## Risk

A Product Case with material unresolved regulatory/security risk may be:
- researchable;
- prototypeable in a safe sandbox;
- blocked from release/build depending on risk.

The risk decision must be explicit.
