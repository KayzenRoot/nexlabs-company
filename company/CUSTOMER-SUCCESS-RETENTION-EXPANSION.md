# NexLabs Technology — Customer Success, Retention & Expansion

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## Customer Success purpose

Customer Success ensures customers realize the value they reasonably expected from the product.

It is not merely support-ticket closure.

## Health dimensions

Depending on product:
- activation;
- usage depth/frequency;
- key workflow success;
- reliability/incidents;
- support burden;
- stakeholder engagement;
- value/outcome evidence;
- renewal timing;
- payment/account status;
- product-fit gaps.

## Health states

- `HEALTHY`
- `WATCH`
- `AT_RISK`
- `RECOVERY_PLAN`
- `CHURNED`

Health must be evidence-backed, not cosmetic scoring.

## Retention

Track:
- logo/customer retention;
- revenue retention;
- cohort retention;
- repeat usage;
- churn reasons.

## Expansion

Expansion is appropriate when:
- value is demonstrated;
- customer has additional legitimate need;
- pricing/tier fit exists;
- support/economics remain healthy.

Do not manufacture expansion through hostage features or surprise limits.

## Churn

Capture:
- reason;
- segment;
- tenure;
- usage/value state;
- price;
- product gaps;
- support/reliability;
- competitor/alternative;
- preventable vs non-preventable judgment.

## Feedback loop

Churn, expansion, objections and success stories feed Product Factory evidence and roadmap decisions.

## References/testimonials

Use customer stories only with permission and accurate context.
