# NexLabs Technology — Institutional Website Information Architecture

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_014`

## Purpose

The institutional website explains:
- who NexLabs is;
- what it explores/builds;
- how it works;
- what public products/projects exist;
- what evidence is available;
- how to use verified public/contact channels.

## V1 core IA

The current website program supports this core:

- `/` — Home
- `/solutions` — capability/solution framing
- `/technology` — technology architecture
- `/research` — research method/directions
- `/company` — company purpose/principles
- `/contact` — verified contact boundary

These routes remain valid public architecture where implemented.

## Portfolio route

`/portfolio` is the canonical future home for public product/project/technology records.

It should not publish empty/fabricated cards simply to make the company look larger.

## Expansion IA

The following may be admitted as evidence/content matures:

- `/products`
- `/portfolio/[slug]`
- `/ai-workforce`
- `/web3`
- `/games`
- `/insights`
- `/careers`
- `/investors`

A planned route is not an implemented route.

## Investor boundary

Public investor content may explain high-level thesis.

Confidential fundraising documents/data-room content require protected access and belong to Investor System governance.

## Contact boundary

A public Contact route does not imply a working form/inbox.

Only verified governed channels may be published.

## Navigation principles

- shallow, understandable structure;
- no dead routes masquerading as available pages;
- accessible semantic navigation;
- mobile parity;
- stable deep links where practical.

## Content-source rule

Corporate truth comes from canonical company sources.

The website repository may adapt presentation/copy but may not silently invent company strategy, proof or portfolio state.
