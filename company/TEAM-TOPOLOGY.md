# NexLabs Technology — Team Topology

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_006`

## 1. Default topology

NexLabs organizes around **capability pools** and temporary **mission pods**.

### Capability pools
Persistent definitions and reusable expertise:
- Product;
- Engineering;
- Research/Data;
- Security/Risk;
- Operations;
- Finance;
- Commercial.

### Mission pods
Temporary cross-functional groups assembled around:
- a Work Order;
- a product increment;
- a research question;
- an incident;
- a launch;
- a commercial experiment.

## 2. Software product pod

Typical composition:

`Product Lead → Architect → Executor(s) → QA → Reviewer/Auditor`

Optional specialists:
- Security;
- Data/Database;
- DevOps;
- UX;
- Web3;
- Game;
- Research;
- Growth.

## 3. Research pod

Typical composition:

`Research Lead → Research Specialist(s) → Evidence/Source Reviewer → Decision handoff`

Research does not directly become company truth without the appropriate decision or Work Order.

## 4. Launch pod

Typical composition:

`Product → Engineering → Security → Operations → Commercial`

Production/high-risk gates remain governed separately.

## 5. Incident pod

Typical composition:

`Incident Coordinator → Operations/SRE → Security → affected Engineering → Founder when required`

Incident authority follows WO-005 and does not erase normal evidence obligations.

## 6. Cross-team dependencies

Each pod must declare:
- objective;
- owner/lead;
- participating roles;
- authoritative sources;
- output;
- review/approval path;
- stop condition.

## 7. Shared specialist rule

A high-skill specialist should normally serve multiple products rather than be duplicated permanently inside every product team.

Examples:
- AppSec;
- Database Architecture;
- Smart Contract Audit;
- SRE;
- Financial Modeling.

## 8. Conflict prevention

Parallel pods may not mutate the same governed state without:
- explicit ownership;
- branch/workspace isolation;
- merge/reconciliation rules;
- conflict detection.

GEF Work Orders remain the engineering boundary for software increments.
