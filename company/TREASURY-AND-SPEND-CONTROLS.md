# NexLabs Technology — Treasury & Spend Controls

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_012`

## Boundary

Treasury analysis and spend planning may be automated.

Actual company money movement is `HIGH_ASSURANCE`.

## Spend request

A material spend request should identify:
- requester/role;
- vendor/counterparty;
- purpose;
- product/cost center;
- amount/currency;
- recurring vs one-time;
- term/renewal;
- budget status;
- alternatives;
- approval class.

## Approval

Exact thresholds will be set by Founder policy later.

Possible future bands:
- auto within bounded preapproved micro-spend;
- auto+audit;
- review required;
- Founder approval;
- prohibited.

## Vendor commitments

Do not treat a card charge as the only commitment.

Track:
- subscriptions;
- contracts;
- minimums;
- usage commitments;
- auto-renewals;
- termination windows.

## Treasury separation

Company operating cash, tax/reserve funds, customer funds and investment/treasury assets should remain logically/accountingly separated as applicable.

## Payments

Execution requires:
- beneficiary verification;
- amount/currency;
- invoice/contract/purpose;
- approval;
- duplicate/idempotency check;
- provider receipt;
- reconciliation.

## No speculative treasury

Company treasury is not used for speculative crypto/trading by default.

Any future treasury investment policy requires a separate decision and risk framework.

## Fraud

Changes to bank/payment details require out-of-band verification proportional to risk.

## Access

Finance analysis agents do not automatically receive banking/payment credentials.
