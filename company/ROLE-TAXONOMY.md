# NexLabs Technology — AI Role Taxonomy

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_006`

## Role families

### 1. Executive / Coordination
Examples:
- Chief of Staff;
- Technology/Engineering Lead;
- Product/Strategy Lead;
- Operations Lead;
- Finance Lead;
- Commercial/Growth Lead;
- Security Lead;
- Research Lead.

Purpose: coordinate functions, not perform every specialist task personally.

### 2. Product
Examples:
- Product Manager;
- Product Analyst;
- UX Researcher;
- UX/UI Designer;
- Product Data Analyst.

### 3. Engineering
Examples:
- Software Architect;
- Backend Engineer;
- Frontend Engineer;
- Mobile Engineer;
- Full-stack Engineer;
- Code Executor;
- Database Engineer;
- API Engineer;
- DevOps/Platform Engineer;
- SRE;
- QA/Test Engineer;
- Reviewer/Auditor;
- Web3 Engineer;
- Smart Contract Engineer;
- Game Engineer;
- Tools/Engine Engineer.

### 4. Research & Data
Examples:
- Technical Researcher;
- Market Researcher;
- Competitive Intelligence Analyst;
- AI/Model Researcher;
- Data Analyst;
- Data Scientist;
- Financial/Market Analyst;
- Blockchain/Crypto Analyst.

### 5. Security & Risk
Examples:
- Application Security Engineer;
- Cloud/Platform Security Analyst;
- Smart Contract Security Reviewer;
- Threat/Risk Analyst;
- Privacy/Compliance Analyst.

Legal/compliance professionals may still be required externally for formal regulated/legal matters.

### 6. Operations
Examples:
- Operations Coordinator;
- Reliability Coordinator;
- Incident Coordinator;
- Knowledge/Documentation Manager;
- Vendor/Tool Operations;
- Automation Specialist.

### 7. Finance
Examples:
- Financial Planning Analyst;
- Unit Economics Analyst;
- Cost/Usage Analyst;
- Investor Metrics Analyst.

These roles analyze and recommend. They do not gain money-movement authority from role name.

### 8. Commercial
Examples:
- Marketing Strategist;
- Content/SEO Specialist;
- Growth Analyst;
- Sales Operations;
- Customer Success;
- Partnership Research.

## Role granularity

A role should be split when:
- different permissions are required;
- different risk/separation rules apply;
- different expertise/evaluation is needed;
- independent review materially improves assurance.

A role should be combined when:
- work is low-risk;
- responsibilities naturally align;
- separate agents would add coordination cost without assurance benefit.

## Role identity rule

Role IDs should eventually be stable, machine-readable and independent of provider/model.

Example future pattern:

`ENG.ARCHITECT`
`ENG.EXECUTOR`
`ENG.QA`
`ENG.REVIEWER`
`SEC.APPSEC`
`FIN.UNIT_ECONOMICS`
