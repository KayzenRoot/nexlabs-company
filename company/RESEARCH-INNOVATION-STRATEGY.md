# NexLabs Technology — Research & Innovation Strategy

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_010`

## Purpose

NexLabs research exists to reduce uncertainty, create reusable knowledge and discover differentiated technical capability that can become product advantage, internal infrastructure, licensable technology or public/open work.

Research is not a decorative R&D label. It must have a question, evidence path, decision consequence and provenance.

## Strategic research categories

### Applied product research
Improves understanding of a product/user/technical problem.

### Platform / infrastructure research
Explores reusable systems, agent orchestration, developer tooling, media systems, data infrastructure and other cross-product capabilities.

### Model / AI systems research
Evaluates models, prompting/orchestration, memory, RAG, evaluation, local inference and agent-system behavior.

### Web3 / cryptographic research
Explores blockchain protocols, smart-contract systems, data/analytics and infrastructure without treating speculation as evidence.

### Games / interactive systems research
Explores rendering, asset generation, procedural systems, AI behavior, worlds, economy tooling and interactive infrastructure.

### Business / market research
May inform product strategy but must remain distinguishable from technical invention/prototype evidence.

## Research principles

- State the question before collecting evidence.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Prefer falsifiable hypotheses.
- Record negative results when they change future decisions.
- Preserve provenance.
- Time-box exploration.
- Move from research to product only through an explicit transfer decision.
- Do not label common integration work as proprietary invention without evidence of meaningful differentiation.
- Do not publish or open-source potentially sensitive/proprietary work by default.

## Research output classes

A research effort may end as:
- reusable internal knowledge;
- experiment/prototype;
- architecture decision;
- Product Factory input;
- proprietary technology candidate;
- licensing candidate;
- patent-review candidate;
- trade-secret treatment candidate;
- open-source candidate;
- publication candidate;
- rejected/deferred hypothesis.

These labels are internal disposition states, not legal conclusions.

## Founder role

Founder/CEO decides strategically material research priorities and disposition of high-value IP candidates.

AI research agents may gather evidence, propose classification and prepare decision packages, but they do not make final legal/IP disposition decisions.
