# NexLabs Technology — Revenue Metrics

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_012`

## Revenue taxonomy

Track separately:
- subscription recurring revenue;
- usage/metered revenue;
- transaction/platform fees;
- licensing;
- enterprise contracted revenue;
- bounded services;
- one-time revenue.

## MRR

MRR represents normalized monthly recurring subscription/contract revenue that is expected to recur under the current agreement/product model.

Exclude by default:
- one-time setup;
- non-recurring services;
- usage revenue with no recurring commitment unless a documented normalization policy exists;
- taxes collected;
- pass-through amounts;
- speculative pipeline.

Annual contracts may be normalized to monthly recurring value for MRR when policy is consistent.

## ARR

Simple run-rate:

`ARR = MRR × 12`

Use only where MRR is meaningful.

ARR is not:
- cash;
- GAAP/statutory revenue;
- total contract value;
- bookings;
- guaranteed future revenue.

## Other metrics

Depending on product:
- ARPU/ARPA;
- paid conversion;
- expansion revenue;
- contraction;
- logo churn;
- revenue churn;
- NRR/GRR;
- cohort revenue;
- usage growth.

## Evidence state

Every metric should carry:
- period;
- source;
- calculation version;
- actual/estimated state;
- confidence;
- last refresh.

## Revenue quality

Revenue quality improves with:
- retention;
- margin;
- diversification;
- low manual burden;
- predictable renewal;
- low fraud/refund risk.

## No vanity aggregation

Do not combine unlike revenue categories into one “MRR” number to make the business look larger.
