# NexLabs Technology — Investor Readiness Operating Model

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Purpose

Investor readiness means NexLabs can explain its strategy, products, economics, risks and capital plan with evidence that withstands diligence.

It is not the same as “having a nice deck”.

## Core loop

`CANONICAL COMPANY STATE → INVESTOR NARRATIVE → CLAIM/EVIDENCE MAP → DILIGENCE PACKAGE → QUESTIONS/GAPS → REMEDIATION → UPDATED INVESTOR STATE`

## Source precedence

Investor materials inherit truth from:
- company strategy;
- business model;
- product/portfolio evidence;
- finance;
- GTM;
- security/privacy;
- public-proof/disclosure;
- legal/corporate records where applicable.

Investor materials may summarize but may not silently override those sources.

## Fact states

Material investor-facing claims use:
- `ACTUAL`
- `FORECAST`
- `SCENARIO`
- `TARGET`
- `UNAVAILABLE`
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`

A claim may also carry proof class:
- `SELF_ATTESTED`
- `MEASURED`
- `THIRD_PARTY_VERIFIED`
- `PERMISSIONED_EXTERNAL`

## Readiness dimensions

- company/strategy clarity;
- product/portfolio evidence;
- market/competitive evidence;
- revenue/economics;
- GTM evidence;
- technology/security;
- IP/provenance;
- financial controls;
- corporate/legal records;
- fundraising use-of-funds/milestones;
- diligence accessibility;
- risk transparency.

## Founder authority

Founder/CEO retains authority over:
- whether/when to raise;
- investor selection;
- ask range;
- negotiation posture;
- disclosure of sensitive information;
- financing instrument/terms;
- final pitch narrative.

AI agents may prepare, model, check consistency and maintain evidence indexes.

## Investor stage

Readiness should be proportional to round/stage.

A pre-revenue company may legitimately have many `UNAVAILABLE` traction metrics while still demonstrating strong product/technology/market evidence.

Unknown is acceptable. Fiction is not.
