# NexLabs Technology — Investor Ask, Use of Funds & Milestones

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Ask model

Before terms exist, the “ask” is a funding scenario, not a binding financing offer.

It should specify:
- target capital range/scenario;
- intended runway;
- primary use-of-funds categories;
- milestone package;
- contingency/downside logic;
- timing assumptions;
- key dependencies.

## Use of funds

Use the canonical Use-of-Funds categories.

Each allocation should connect:
`CAPITAL → CAPABILITY / ACTIVITY → MILESTONE → EVIDENCE → NEXT DECISION`

## Milestones

Good investor milestones reduce uncertainty around:
- product;
- market;
- GTM;
- economics;
- technology;
- operations/governance.

## Tranches

If financing is tranche-based:
- amount/release is not triggered by time alone;
- objective evidence is required;
- failure enters replan/review;
- binding investor changes require actual agreement/legal handling.

## Example milestone dimensions

- product launch/readiness;
- validated activation/retention;
- paying-customer range;
- recurring revenue range;
- gross margin/cost-to-serve;
- reliability/security gate;
- Company OS/autonomous-engineering capability;
- portfolio milestone.

These are categories, not current NexLabs claims.

## Funding amount

No canonical numeric ask is frozen until the Founder selects a scenario from current finance/roadmap evidence.

## Runway

Funding amount should be modeled against:
- opening cash;
- burn;
- committed spend;
- planned capacity;
- GTM spend;
- contingency;
- milestone timing.

## No forced spending

Raising capital does not make the full budget mandatory to spend.

Capital allocation can change as evidence changes, subject to financing obligations/approvals.
