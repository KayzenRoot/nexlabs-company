# NexLabs Technology — Research Lifecycle

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_010`

## Canonical states

`QUESTION`  
A bounded research question exists.

`INTAKE`  
Purpose, owner, scope, sources and decision consequence are defined.

`RESEARCH`  
External/internal evidence gathering is active.

`EVIDENCE`  
Evidence set is sufficiently structured for analysis.

`HYPOTHESIS`  
A testable explanation/technical proposition is stated.

`EXPERIMENT`  
A controlled test/benchmark is executed.

`PROTOTYPE`  
A bounded artifact demonstrates a technical possibility.

`REVIEW`  
Evidence, method, provenance and implications are reviewed.

`DISPOSITION`  
Founder/authorized governance chooses what happens next.

`TRANSFER`  
Approved knowledge/technology moves into Product Factory, engineering, internal platform, licensing, open-source or publication process.

`DEFERRED`  
Question remains potentially useful but inactive.

`REJECTED`  
Evidence does not justify continued work.

`ARCHIVED`  
Research is retained as historical knowledge.

## Transition rule

Every transition should record:
- research ID;
- previous/new state;
- decision actor;
- date;
- evidence refs;
- assumptions/unknowns;
- next action.

## Prototype rule

`PROTOTYPE` proves only the scoped technical hypothesis it actually tested.

It does not automatically prove:
- user demand;
- product-market fit;
- production reliability;
- security;
- economics;
- patentability;
- legal ownership.

## Negative results

A failed hypothesis may be a successful research outcome if it reduces uncertainty and is documented honestly.

## Transfer rule

Research cannot silently become production work.

A transfer into product/engineering requires an explicit transfer package and then normal Product Factory / GEF admission.
