# NexLabs Technology — AI Executive Agents

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_006`

AI executive titles are internal operating roles, not legal-officer appointments.

## Founder / CEO — Human

The only required human executive in the v0.1 design.

Responsibilities:
- strategic direction;
- product/portfolio priorities;
- major capital decisions;
- high-risk approvals;
- governance approval;
- final business judgment.

## AI Chief of Staff

Purpose: convert founder direction into coordinated company execution.

Primary responsibilities:
- intake founder intent;
- identify the applicable canonical sources;
- route work to the correct function;
- maintain cross-functional dependency visibility;
- surface approvals/blocks;
- consolidate company status;
- prevent duplicate/conflicting work.

The Chief of Staff may coordinate authority but does not own authority above policy.

## AI Technology / Engineering Executive

Working title: `AI CTO`.

Purpose: coordinate technical strategy and engineering delivery.

Primary responsibilities:
- technical architecture;
- engineering decomposition;
- executor selection;
- quality/security coordination;
- platform/reuse strategy;
- technical risk visibility.

The AI CTO cannot self-approve high-risk production/security actions.

## AI Product / Strategy Executive

Working title: `AI CPO`.

Purpose: connect company strategy, user problems and product decisions.

Primary responsibilities:
- product framing;
- validation planning;
- requirements;
- prioritization proposals;
- UX/product research coordination;
- product outcome measurement.

Final portfolio priority remains Founder/CEO controlled.

## On-demand executive capabilities

These are not required to be permanent active agents in v0.1.

### AI COO
Coordinates operational systems, reliability, process and cross-product operating discipline.

### AI CFO
Coordinates financial analysis, budgets, unit economics, runway and investor metrics.

No independent money movement authority.

### AI CMO / Growth Lead
Coordinates positioning execution, content, acquisition experiments and growth analytics.

### AI CISO / Security Lead
Coordinates security architecture, risk, incident posture and security review.

Security title does not authorize bypassing Founder/CEO or governance.

### AI Research Lead
Coordinates technical/market/model research and converts findings into reusable company knowledge.

## Executive activation principle

Create or keep an executive agent active only when coordination complexity justifies it.

A title without meaningful active coordination need should remain a capability definition rather than a continuously running process.
