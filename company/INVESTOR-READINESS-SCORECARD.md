# NexLabs Technology — Investor Readiness Scorecard

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Purpose

The scorecard prevents “fundraising ready” from becoming a subjective label.

## Dimensions

Each dimension may be:
- `GREEN`
- `YELLOW`
- `RED`
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`

### A. Company thesis
Clear, consistent, evidence-aware.

### B. Problem / market
ICP/problem evidence and credible market sizing method.

### C. Product / portfolio
Status/proof/demos accurately represented.

### D. Business model
Revenue/value capture coherent.

### E. GTM
Channel/sales/retention evidence appropriate to stage.

### F. Finance
Cash/burn/runway/budget and forecast available enough for stage.

### G. Unit economics
Available or explicitly immature/unavailable.

### H. Technology / AI-native capability
Architecture/evidence differentiated and understandable.

### I. Security / privacy
Baseline governance/evidence sufficient for stage/risk.

### J. IP / legal
Ownership/provenance and key legal/corporate records sufficiently organized.

### K. Cap table / corporate records
Authoritative current records available in protected diligence.

### L. Ask / use-of-funds / milestones
Capital request tied to a coherent plan.

### M. Data room
Indexed, current, permissioned and access-controlled.

### N. Risk disclosure
Material risks identified with controls/owners.

## Blocking RED conditions

NexLabs cannot mark itself `INVESTOR_READY` when any applicable critical item is RED, including examples such as:
- corporate ownership/cap table unknown when an equity raise is contemplated;
- no credible cash/burn/runway view;
- material investor claim unsupported;
- material IP ownership dispute/unknown;
- critical security/privacy blocker relevant to the raise/product;
- ask has no use-of-funds/milestone logic;
- confidential data room has no access-control plan;
- material legal financing path is undefined when binding terms are being pursued.

## Readiness states

- `NOT_READY`
- `PREPARING`
- `READY_FOR_INITIAL_CONVERSATIONS`
- `READY_FOR_DILIGENCE`
- `READY_FOR_TERM_NEGOTIATION`

These states describe preparation, not fundraising success.

## Score evidence

Each dimension records:
- status;
- evidence refs;
- gaps;
- owner;
- next action;
- last reviewed.

## No weighted vanity score

A single “92/100” can hide one catastrophic red item.

Critical blockers override aggregate scoring.
