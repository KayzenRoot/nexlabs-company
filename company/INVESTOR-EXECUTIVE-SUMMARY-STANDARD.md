# NexLabs Technology — Investor Executive Summary Standard

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Purpose

The Executive Summary provides a short decision-quality view of NexLabs without requiring the reader to decode a full deck.

## Recommended structure

### 1. Company
Who NexLabs is and what category/thesis it represents.

### 2. Problem / opportunity
The recurring high-value friction or market condition the company is positioned to address.

### 3. Solution / company capability
How NexLabs creates products and why the operating model matters.

### 4. Products / portfolio
Only evidence-backed current products/projects/status.

### 5. Business model
How value is expected/known to be captured.

### 6. Market / ICP
Current market thesis, ICPs and evidence state.

### 7. Differentiation
Technology, product, distribution or operating-system advantages with evidence level.

### 8. Traction / proof
Actual verified traction only; if unavailable, describe current evidence/milestones instead.

### 9. Economics / financial state
Cash/runway/revenue/unit economics only when available and appropriately classified.

### 10. Funding plan
Ask scenario/range, use of funds and milestone logic, explicitly non-binding until real financing terms exist.

### 11. Risks / gaps
Material unresolved risks or evidence gaps.

### 12. Contact / next diligence step
Use verified governed channel only.

## Claim formatting

Material metrics should include:
- state;
- period/date;
- source;
- last verification.

## Forbidden compression

Do not hide uncertainty by:
- merging targets with actuals;
- calling pipeline revenue;
- calling prototypes “products in market”;
- calling estimated market share “traction”;
- omitting material caveats necessary to understand a number.

## Length

Optimize for decision clarity, not a fixed page count.
