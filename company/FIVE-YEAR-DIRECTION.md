# NexLabs Technology — Five-Year Direction

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_003`  
**Nature:** Strategic target, not a claim of present capability.

The periods below are directional planning horizons, not promised calendar deadlines.

## Horizon 0 — Foundation

Goal: build the company operating substrate.

Targets:
- canonical company strategy and governance;
- AI workforce model;
- Product Factory;
- Company OS architecture;
- local-first agent runtime;
- first governed autonomous engineering cell;
- founder visibility and auditability.

Success signal: the founder can initiate a product increment and observe a governed end-to-end execution loop with reliable evidence.

## Horizon 1 — Repeatable product creation

Goal: prove that the Product Factory works on real product opportunities.

Targets:
- validate and operate a small number of focused products;
- measure acquisition, retention, cost and product quality;
- identify reusable technologies and workflows;
- improve agent roles through evaluation rather than ad hoc prompts;
- establish disciplined product pause/kill/scale decisions.

Success signal: product creation becomes a repeatable company capability rather than a one-off project.

## Horizon 2 — Portfolio operating leverage

Goal: make successful products and internal capability reinforce each other.

Targets:
- shared observability, billing/integration patterns and operational tooling where justified;
- portfolio-level research and distribution learning;
- stronger proprietary internal technology;
- greater autonomous operation within approved bounds;
- reduced founder involvement in routine execution while preserving strategic control.

Success signal: each additional qualified product can reuse meaningful company capability instead of rebuilding the company around itself.

## Horizon 3 — Durable AI-native technology company

Goal: establish NexLabs as a durable operator of technology products with a highly leveraged AI-native workforce.

Targets:
- a portfolio containing proven products rather than a large count of experiments;
- multiple validated revenue/value engines if supported by product evidence;
- robust company memory, auditability and security;
- production-grade agent governance;
- optional externalization/licensing of internal technology when strategically justified;
- infrastructure scaled according to real operating needs.

Success signal: NexLabs can reliably research, build, launch, operate, improve and retire products through a controlled company system.

## Five-year north star

**A founder-directed technology company whose governed AI workforce can operate much of the product lifecycle while quality, security, evidence, capital and authority remain under explicit control.**

No revenue, valuation, headcount, customer or market-share target is frozen in WO-003. Those belong to later financial, commercial and investor Work Orders where evidence and assumptions can be managed properly.
