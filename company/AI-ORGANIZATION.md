# NexLabs Technology — AI Organization

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_006`

## 1. Company shape

NexLabs is a **one-founder, AI-native organization**.

The human layer is intentionally small:

`Founder / CEO`

The digital operating layer is elastic:

`AI Coordination Core → Functional Leads → Specialist Agents → Tools / Executors`

The organization is designed to scale by **activating governed capabilities**, not by permanently spawning a large number of idle agents.

## 3. Always-on vs elastic

### Minimal always-on core
For v0.1, the intended core is:
- Founder / CEO;
- AI Chief of Staff;
- AI Product / Strategy coordinator;
- AI Technology / Engineering coordinator.

Other functions are activated when the current Work Order, product, risk or operating state requires them.

### Elastic specialists
Specialists are activated for bounded work such as:
- security review;
- market research;
- financial modeling;
- database architecture;
- smart-contract analysis;
- game systems;
- growth analysis;
- production operations.

When work ends, the specialist may become inactive while its governed state/evidence remains available.

## 4. Role titles are not authority

“CTO”, “CFO”, “CISO”, “Head of Product” or similar AI titles describe **coordination responsibility**.

They do not:
- confer legal corporate office;
- grant tool permissions;
- authorize spending;
- authorize signing;
- bypass review;
- override Founder/CEO authority;
- override canonical governance.

Authority comes from the governance system, not the title.

## 5. Product teams

A product may temporarily assemble a cross-functional pod such as:

`Product Lead + Architect + Executor + QA + Reviewer + Security + GTM specialist`

The pod dissolves or changes when the work changes. Company capability remains reusable across products.

## 6. Organizational objective

The organization is successful when the Founder can direct outcomes rather than manually coordinate every specialist, while still retaining visibility, approval control and trustworthy evidence.
