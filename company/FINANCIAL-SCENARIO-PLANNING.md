# NexLabs Technology — Financial Scenario Planning

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_012`

## Required scenarios

At minimum:
- `DOWNSIDE`
- `BASE`
- `UPSIDE`

Optional:
- no-funding;
- delayed-funding;
- high-inference-cost;
- slower-revenue;
- major-provider-price-change;
- incident/regulatory cost.

## Scenario inputs

- opening cash;
- revenue growth/retention;
- collection timing;
- model/cloud cost;
- fixed operating cost;
- marketing/GTM;
- staffing/capacity;
- one-time costs;
- financing;
- contingency.

## Scenario outputs

- monthly ending cash;
- burn;
- runway;
- gross margin;
- milestone timing;
- funding need/window;
- critical assumptions.

## Assumption sensitivity

Identify the variables that matter most.

Examples:
- paid conversion;
- churn;
- AI cost per successful task;
- CAC;
- gross margin;
- hiring timing.

## Triggered action

Scenario planning is useful only if it defines decisions.

Example:
“If downside runway crosses approved threshold, pause Explore builds and discretionary GTM before cutting critical security.”

Numeric thresholds remain Founder-approved policy.

## No false precision

Long-range monthly numbers with weak evidence should not look more precise than the assumptions justify.

Use ranges and confidence where appropriate.
