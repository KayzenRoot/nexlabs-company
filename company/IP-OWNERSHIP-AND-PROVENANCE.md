# NexLabs Technology — IP Ownership & Provenance

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_010`

## Purpose

Preserve a traceable chain showing where NexLabs code, designs, research and inventions came from and what rights/constraints may apply.

This policy does not replace formal legal analysis.

## Provenance layers

For material assets record:
- creator/contributor;
- date/time;
- project/research ID;
- commit/artifact ID;
- AI-assisted generation details when material;
- source/reference material;
- third-party code/models/data;
- license/terms;
- customer/partner constraints;
- public disclosure state.

## Company-created work

NexLabs should use written agreements and appropriate legal structures to establish ownership/assignment for human contractors/employees/partners when they are added.

A repository commit alone is not a complete IP assignment mechanism.

## Founder-created work

Company formation/legal counsel should document how pre-company or founder-created IP is assigned/licensed to the legal entity when appropriate.

Until formalized, the repository should avoid overstating legal ownership.

## AI-assisted work

AI-generated/assisted content requires:
- provider terms review where material;
- source/provenance hygiene;
- human/company review;
- avoidance of false legal claims about protectability.

The organization owns its governed artifacts operationally, but legal copyright/patent treatment must be assessed under applicable law/facts.

## Third-party software

For dependencies:
- record package/repository/version;
- license;
- material notices/obligations;
- modifications;
- distribution implications.

Avoid introducing incompatible licenses into proprietary components without review.

## Models and data

Record:
- model/provider/license/terms;
- dataset source/license/consent constraints;
- commercial-use limits;
- redistribution/training restrictions where relevant.

## Confidential information

Do not ingest customer/partner confidential material into external models/tools unless policy/contract permits it.

## Disputes/unknowns

Unknown ownership/provenance is `BLOCKED` for:
- strong proprietary claims;
- public release;
- outbound licensing;
- disclosure requiring clear rights.
