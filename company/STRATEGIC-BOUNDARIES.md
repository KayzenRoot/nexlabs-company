# NexLabs Technology — Strategic Boundaries

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_003`

Boundaries protect focus. They may evolve through explicit decisions, not through incidental project drift.

## We will

- build focused software products where technology can remove meaningful friction;
- use AI extensively inside the company when authority and evidence are clear;
- maintain provider independence for critical company capability;
- use a portfolio model when products benefit from shared internal capability;
- prefer local/low-cost infrastructure during exploration when it meets the requirement;
- create reusable internal technology when reuse is justified;
- require stronger assurance for security, finance, Web3 and irreversible operations;
- keep product shutdown, pause and pivot as legitimate portfolio decisions.

## We will not

- operate as an unbounded generic development agency by default;
- create products solely because a trend is fashionable;
- launch weak clones simply to maximize the number of products;
- let an AI employee expand its own permissions or rewrite governance;
- give autonomous agents unrestricted control of company funds, crypto or signing keys;
- bind Company OS to Hermes, Codex, OpenAI or another single vendor;
- fabricate customers, revenue, growth, partnerships, patents, staff or market position;
- treat prototypes as production systems;
- move confidential material into the current public repository.

## Regulated and high-risk spaces

NexLabs may research and build tooling related to financial markets, crypto and Web3. Actual trading, custody, money movement, financial advice, on-chain value execution or other regulated activity requires separate risk, legal/compliance and authorization decisions before operation.

## Product admission boundary

A product should eventually prove:
- a concrete user/problem hypothesis;
- strategic fit;
- feasible distribution;
- technical feasibility;
- acceptable legal/security risk;
- a path to value capture or strategically valuable learning.

Exact scoring and kill/continue rules belong to WO-008.
