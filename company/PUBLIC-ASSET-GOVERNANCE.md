# NexLabs Technology — Public Asset Governance

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_014`

## Covered assets

- logo/wordmark;
- icons;
- 3D models;
- images;
- screenshots;
- diagrams;
- video;
- audio;
- fonts;
- generated media;
- customer/partner logos;
- downloadable documents.

## Asset record

Material public assets should preserve:
- asset ID/path;
- source/creator;
- creation date;
- license/terms;
- AI-generated/assisted status where relevant;
- source prompt/reference provenance when material;
- approved use;
- confidentiality state;
- version/hash for canonical brand masters.

## Logo master

The flat/vector Precision Blades N geometry is the identity master.

Derived:
- monochrome;
- lockups;
- favicon simplification;
- enhanced chrome;
- animated treatments

must remain traceable to the master.

## Third-party assets

Do not publish an asset merely because it was downloadable.

Confirm:
- license;
- commercial/public usage;
- attribution;
- modification rights;
- trademark/privacy/personality considerations.

## AI-generated media

Review for:
- brand fidelity;
- unintended copyrighted/trademarked elements;
- misleading realism;
- factual implication;
- privacy/confidential content.

## Screenshots

Before publication:
- remove secrets/tokens;
- remove private personal/customer data;
- verify environment;
- verify current product status;
- label concept/mockup when not functional product evidence.

## Customer/partner marks

Use only with real relationship, permission/terms and correct status.

## Fonts

Production font families require appropriate web/brand licensing.

## Retention

Keep canonical masters and important exported derivatives versioned/reproducible where practical.
