# NexLabs Technology — Funding & Investment Model

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_012`

## Purpose

Funding exists to accelerate validated company value creation, not to hide weak economics.

## Funding sources

Potential categories:
- founder/self-funded;
- operating revenue;
- grants/prizes/hackathons;
- angel/seed equity;
- convertible/SAFE-like instruments where legally appropriate;
- strategic investment;
- venture capital;
- debt when appropriate;
- non-dilutive partnerships.

No instrument is canonical merely because it is listed.

## Decision package

Before fundraising, define:
- why capital is needed;
- amount range/scenarios;
- runway target;
- use of funds;
- milestones;
- risks;
- current traction/evidence;
- capital efficiency;
- alternatives;
- dilution/obligation implications.

## Legal/financial terms

Valuation, equity %, liquidation rights, board rights, investor protections, debt terms and convertible mechanics require Founder plus qualified legal/accounting review.

AI agents may model scenarios but cannot bind the company.

## Funding scenario

Each scenario should show:
- opening cash;
- funding inflow;
- monthly burn assumptions;
- hiring/capacity assumptions;
- product/GTM spend;
- contingency;
- milestone dates;
- expected ending cash/runway.

## Capital discipline

Raising more capital is not automatically better.

Evaluate:
- dilution/control;
- speed;
- expected return on capital;
- runway;
- execution capacity;
- market timing;
- dependency risk.

## Investor return

Investor economic return depends on the legal instrument and future enterprise outcomes, not scheduled repayment by default for equity.

Any promised return/repayment requires the actual legal financing terms.

## Truthfulness

Fundraising materials clearly separate:
- actual traction;
- forecast;
- scenario;
- target/goal.
