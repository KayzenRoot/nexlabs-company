# NexLabs Technology — Minimum Viable AI Workforce

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_006`

## Principle

The minimum viable workforce is the **smallest governed set of capabilities** that lets the Founder plan, build, review and operate early NexLabs increments.

It is not the full org chart.

## Human

### 1. Founder / CEO
Required.

## AI core

### 2. AI Chief of Staff
Coordinates founder intent, Work Orders, dependencies, approvals and status.

### 3. AI Product / Strategy
Handles problem framing, requirements, product planning and research coordination.

### 4. AI Technology / Engineering
Handles architecture, technical decomposition and engineering coordination.

These three logical roles may initially run through the same capable orchestration platform if their role boundaries remain explicit.

## Engineering cell capabilities

The minimum cell must provide:
- Planner / Architect;
- Executor;
- QA / Test;
- Reviewer / Auditor.

These may be instantiated on demand and do not require four permanently running processes.

## On-demand mandatory specialists

Activate only when the work needs them:
- Security reviewer;
- DevOps/Platform;
- Database/Data;
- UX;
- Market/Research;
- Finance/Unit Economics;
- Commercial/Growth.

Domain-specific:
- Web3/Smart Contracts;
- Financial/Market analysis;
- Game engineering.

## v0.1 workforce target

The v0.1 company can therefore operate with:
- 1 human Founder;
- ~3 persistent logical AI coordination capabilities;
- 4 core software-delivery capabilities instantiated as needed;
- a specialist pool activated on demand.

This is a capability model, not a promise of a fixed number of model processes or paid seats.

## Why not dozens of permanent agents

Too many continuously active agents create:
- duplicated context;
- contradictory decisions;
- higher cost;
- harder audit;
- stale memory;
- coordination overhead;
- unnecessary permission surface.

NexLabs scales by adding **justified capabilities**, not by maximizing agent count.
