# NexLabs Technology — Lifecycle Marketing

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## Lifecycle

Possible stages:
- visitor/prospect;
- lead;
- qualified;
- trial/free user;
- activated;
- paid;
- retained;
- expansion-ready;
- at-risk;
- churned;
- win-back eligible.

Not every product uses every stage.

## Messaging purpose by stage

### Acquisition
Clarify problem/value.

### Onboarding
Help user reach value quickly.

### Activation
Reinforce the critical workflow.

### Retention
Support recurring value and product education.

### Expansion
Present relevant higher-value use only when fit exists.

### Risk
Help address real blockers before churn.

### Win-back
Reconnect only when there is a credible new reason/value.

## Contact policy

Lifecycle messaging must respect:
- consent/legal basis;
- preference/unsubscribe controls;
- frequency;
- channel expectations;
- suppression lists.

## Personalization

Use the minimum data necessary.

Sensitive/personal data should not be used for manipulative targeting.

## Dark-pattern prohibition

Do not use:
- fake urgency;
- hidden cancellation friction;
- deceptive opt-ins;
- shame messaging;
- fabricated scarcity.

## Measurement

Measure lifecycle impact on activation, retention, expansion and customer value, not only opens/clicks.
