# NexLabs Technology — Commercial Metrics & Attribution

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## Funnel metrics

Depending on model:
- qualified traffic;
- lead rate;
- qualification rate;
- trial/start rate;
- activation;
- paid conversion;
- opportunity win rate;
- sales cycle;
- onboarding completion;
- retention/churn;
- expansion;
- NRR/GRR;
- CAC/payback.

## Funnel integrity

Define numerator, denominator, time window and cohort.

Do not compare incompatible cohorts as one trend.

## Attribution

Attribution is an estimation system.

Possible models:
- first touch;
- last touch;
- multi-touch;
- self-reported;
- incrementality/experiments.

No single model is automatically ground truth.

## Attribution record

Include:
- source/channel;
- model;
- confidence;
- known blind spots;
- period;
- campaign/experiment where applicable.

## CAC linkage

Channel CAC should eventually connect to:
- activation;
- retention;
- gross/contribution margin;
- payback.

Cheap leads that never activate are not efficient acquisition.

## Revenue retention

Where applicable:

`GRR = (starting recurring revenue - churn - contraction) / starting recurring revenue`

`NRR = (starting recurring revenue - churn - contraction + expansion) / starting recurring revenue`

Definitions must remain consistent.

## Confidence

Commercial metrics may be:
- `MEASURED`
- `ESTIMATED`
- `UNAVAILABLE`
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`

## Privacy

Measurement/attribution remains subject to privacy/consent constraints.
