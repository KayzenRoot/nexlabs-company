# NexLabs Technology — Finance Operating Model

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_012`

## Purpose

Finance exists to keep NexLabs solvent, truthful and able to allocate scarce capital toward the highest-value validated work.

The finance system is a decision system, not just bookkeeping.

## Financial truth states

Every material financial figure should carry one state:

- `ACTUAL` — observed and reconciled;
- `COMMITTED` — contractually/operationally committed but not yet fully paid/recognized;
- `FORECAST` — expected future value based on assumptions;
- `SCENARIO` — hypothetical planning case;
- `ASSUMPTION` — input used by a model;
- `UNAVAILABLE` — should exist, but is not yet known;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Unknown is never coerced to zero.

## Core views

NexLabs should eventually maintain:
- cash position;
- monthly cash flow;
- gross burn;
- net burn;
- runway;
- revenue by product/type;
- MRR/ARR where applicable;
- gross and contribution margin;
- AI/cloud/data costs;
- product-level unit economics;
- budget vs actual;
- committed spend;
- funding runway;
- milestone status.

## Accounting boundary

Management finance is not statutory accounting.

Books, tax treatment, revenue recognition, payroll and legal-entity reporting require the appropriate accountant/legal framework.

Internal management metrics may differ from statutory statements, but differences must be traceable.

## Founder authority

Founder/CEO retains final authority over:
- annual/quarterly budget;
- fundraising;
- material spend;
- treasury;
- financing terms;
- allocation between products;
- shutdown/cost-cut actions.

AI finance roles may analyze, forecast and recommend. They do not move money merely because they produced the analysis.

## Financial cadence

Suggested future cadence:
- daily/near-real-time cash and anomaly visibility;
- weekly cash/burn/commitment review;
- monthly close/management report;
- quarterly budget/portfolio reforecast;
- event-driven review after funding, major launch, incident or large variance.

Actual cadence may adapt to company stage.

## Control principle

No financial dashboard is trusted if source freshness, reconciliation status or assumptions are unknown.
