# NexLabs Technology — Use of Funds

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_012`

## Purpose

Every funding plan maps capital to measurable company capability/outcome.

## Suggested categories

- product/engineering;
- AI/model/cloud/data;
- security/compliance;
- GTM/distribution;
- customer success/support;
- legal/accounting/corporate;
- research/IP;
- talent/contractors;
- equipment/infrastructure;
- contingency/reserve.

## Each allocation records

- category;
- amount or %;
- time window;
- intended outcome;
- owner;
- milestone linkage;
- spend gate;
- downside adjustment.

## Allocation is not entitlement

A planned budget does not require the company to spend it.

Capital may be reallocated when evidence changes, subject to Founder/investor/legal obligations.

## Product funding

Product allocations should consider:
- validation state;
- economics;
- distribution;
- risk;
- strategic reuse;
- support burden.

Avoid equal funding merely for fairness between projects.

## Contingency

Maintain a contingency/reserve appropriate to stage/risk rather than planning cash to zero.

## Reporting

Investor reporting should compare:
- planned use;
- actual use;
- variance;
- milestone result;
- updated forecast.

No retroactive relabeling solely to make a use-of-funds plan appear met.
