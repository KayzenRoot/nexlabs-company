# NexLabs Technology — Sales Pipeline & Qualification

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## Pipeline stages

Baseline B2B/assisted flow:

`NEW → CONTACTED → DISCOVERY → QUALIFIED → SOLUTION_FIT → PROPOSAL/TRIAL → COMMIT/PROCUREMENT → WON / LOST`

Product-led flows may use a different pipeline while preserving explicit transitions.

## Stage rule

A stage represents evidence, not optimism.

Every stage has:
- entry criteria;
- required evidence/activity;
- exit criteria;
- next action;
- owner.

## Qualification dimensions

Evaluate:
- ICP fit;
- problem/pain;
- urgency/trigger;
- current alternative;
- desired outcome;
- product fit;
- technical/security fit;
- decision authority/process;
- budget/economic fit where relevant;
- timing;
- risks/disqualifiers.

## Qualified

“Qualified” means there is enough evidence to justify commercial effort.

It does not mean “they replied”.

## Lost reason

Capture standardized lost reasons:
- no problem/priority;
- no fit;
- price/economics;
- competitor/alternative;
- security/compliance;
- timing;
- internal/no decision;
- product gap;
- sales execution;
- unknown.

## Commitments

Sales may not promise:
- unsupported roadmap dates;
- custom features without approval;
- security/compliance status not held;
- contract terms beyond authority.

## Forecast

Sales stage probability is an estimate, not booked revenue or cash.
