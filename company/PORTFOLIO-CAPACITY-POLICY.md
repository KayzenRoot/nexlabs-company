# NexLabs Technology — Portfolio Capacity Policy

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_008`

## Purpose

NexLabs can generate ideas faster than it can responsibly validate, build, launch and operate them.

Capacity policy prevents idea abundance from becoming execution sprawl.

## Work-in-progress principle

Active WIP is deliberately limited.

Categories:
- research/validation WIP;
- product build WIP;
- launch/observation WIP;
- operational maintenance WIP.

Exact numeric limits may evolve with measured company throughput.

## Initial operating default

Until Company OS measures real throughput:

- keep **very few** products in active build simultaneously;
- prefer finishing/learning from an admitted build before adding another;
- allow more cheap research/validation experiments than production builds;
- do not start a new build merely because an executor is temporarily available.

## Capacity includes more than coding

A product consumes:
- Founder decision attention;
- research;
- review/security;
- testing;
- deployment;
- observability;
- support;
- marketing/distribution;
- finance/ops;
- maintenance.

An idle code executor does not mean the company has product capacity.

## Admission question

Before admitting new build WIP:

**What existing work will slow down, pause or lose attention if this starts now?**

If the answer is unknown, capacity has not been assessed.

## Expedite

Urgent work may preempt WIP for:
- security;
- outage;
- contractual/user harm;
- critical dependency.

“Exciting new idea” is not an expedite class.

## WIP expansion

Increase simultaneous build capacity only when evidence shows:
- delivery throughput is stable;
- quality does not degrade;
- Founder approval load remains manageable;
- review/security/ops are not bottlenecks;
- cost remains acceptable.

## WIP reduction

Reduce active portfolio when:
- completion time rises;
- blocked work accumulates;
- reviews lag;
- incidents/support rise;
- founder attention fragments;
- products remain unobserved after launch.
