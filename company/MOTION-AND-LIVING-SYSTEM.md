# NexLabs Technology — Motion & Living System

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_014`

## Goal

Digital experiences should feel like one connected technological organism.

“Living” means controlled circulation and response, not constant spectacle.

## Motion vocabulary

Preferred:
- slow travelling light along structures/mark edges;
- subtle network/energy filaments;
- restrained particles/depth;
- gentle reflection drift;
- small pointer parallax;
- scroll-linked reveal/depth transitions;
- local illumination response on interactive elements;
- smooth handoff between sections.

## Precision Blades N

Allowed:
- short assembly/reveal;
- one controlled light sweep;
- subtle hover depth;
- nearly imperceptible idle reflection.

Not allowed:
- continuous spin;
- aggressive pulse;
- noisy glitch loop;
- navigation-blocking intro.

## One-world continuity

Motion may connect sections through shared energy/lighting language so the page feels continuous rather than a stack of isolated templates.

## Motion tiers

### LIVE
Full approved environmental motion where device/performance supports it.

### REDUCED
Lower density/amplitude and fewer effects.

### STATIC
Poster/static composition preserves hierarchy, identity and content.

The product/site must remain usable in STATIC.

## Reduced motion

`prefers-reduced-motion` must remove nonessential movement.

Reduced motion is a first-class design state, not an afterthought.

## Interaction restraint

Pointer/parallax should be measured in a few pixels/degrees, not aggressive camera control.

No motion may:
- delay navigation;
- make text unreadable;
- create focus loss;
- be required to understand a claim;
- trap scrolling.

## Performance

Poster-first rendering and progressive enhancement are preferred for expensive scenes.

If fidelity and usability/performance conflict, preserve semantic content and recognizable brand first.
