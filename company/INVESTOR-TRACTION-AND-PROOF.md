# NexLabs Technology — Investor Traction & Proof

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_015`

## Purpose

Traction is evidence that reduces important business uncertainty.

It is not a collage of large numbers.

## Proof categories

### Product evidence
- prototypes accepted;
- activation;
- usage;
- retention;
- reliability;
- product validation.

### Commercial evidence
- paid customers;
- recurring revenue;
- conversion;
- pipeline quality;
- sales cycles;
- partnerships with actual agreements/permission.

### Economic evidence
- gross margin;
- cost-to-serve;
- CAC/payback;
- runway;
- revenue quality.

### Technology evidence
- benchmarks;
- shipped systems;
- test/evaluation outcomes;
- reuse;
- operational automation evidence.

### External validation
- awards;
- grants;
- hackathon results;
- publications;
- certifications;
- partner/customer references

only when independently verifiable and appropriately permissioned.

## Traction states

- `ACTUAL_VERIFIED`
- `EXTERNAL_VERIFIED`
- `FORECAST`
- `TARGET`
- `UNAVAILABLE`

Forecast/target are not called traction.

## Pre-revenue companies

If revenue/customers are unavailable, use honest evidence such as:
- product completion;
- validation interviews;
- technical benchmarks;
- pilot interest, clearly labeled;
- waitlist, with quality caveats;
- launch readiness;
- proprietary capability.

Do not convert “interest” into customers.

## Pipeline

Pipeline requires:
- stage;
- source;
- opportunity evidence;
- expected value/range;
- probability methodology;
- close window.

Pipeline is not revenue.

## Social proof

Use customer/partner logos, quotes and case studies only under Public Proof rules.

## Negative evidence

Investor readiness improves when meaningful failed experiments and learned constraints are understood, not hidden.
