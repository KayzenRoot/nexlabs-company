# NexLabs Technology — Recurring Revenue Model

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_004`

## Objective

Build revenue streams whose value and economics can repeat without proportional manual effort.

## Preferred recurring structures

### Subscription
Fixed recurring entitlement for ongoing product value.

### Subscription + included usage
Recurring access with a defined included allowance.

### Subscription + overage
Base recurring fee plus metered consumption beyond included usage.

### Prepaid credits with recurring replenishment
Useful where consumption is irregular but repeatable, especially with variable AI/compute cost.

### Contracted enterprise capacity
Recurring commitment for seats, capacity, governance, support or service levels.

## Recurrence quality

Recurring billing alone is not recurring value.

A healthy recurring model eventually requires evidence that customers:
- keep receiving value;
- retain or repeat usage;
- accept continued payment;
- can be served with acceptable economics.

## Annual plans

Annual prepayment may improve cash predictability and retention, but should normally follow evidence that the product has durable value. Discounts must be economically explicit.

## Churn and retention

Retention is a first-class product signal. Later product/finance systems should distinguish:
- logo/customer retention;
- revenue retention;
- usage retention;
- cohort behavior;
- voluntary vs involuntary churn.

## Expansion

Expansion may come from:
- more seats;
- more usage;
- higher-value capability;
- additional products/modules;
- enterprise requirements.

Expansion should result from additional value, not dark-pattern friction.

## Revenue recognition / accounting

Formal accounting treatment is jurisdiction/entity-specific and belongs to professional accounting/finance implementation, not this strategy document.
