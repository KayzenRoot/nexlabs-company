# NexLabs Technology — Positioning & Value Proposition

**Status:** `CANONICAL_WO_013`

## Positioning inputs

Positioning must derive from:
- ICP;
- problem evidence;
- product capability;
- differentiation;
- alternative/competition;
- proof;
- pricing/economics;
- risk constraints.

## Positioning frame

For a defined target:
- problem/context;
- category/frame;
- primary value;
- why NexLabs/product is different;
- proof/evidence;
- limitations when material.

## Value proposition

A value proposition should answer:
- what outcome is improved;
- for whom;
- under what conditions;
- why the product is credible.

## Claims rule

Do not claim:
- unsupported ROI;
- guaranteed outcomes;
- nonexistent capability;
- market leadership without evidence;
- customer logos/testimonials without permission/evidence;
- security/compliance certification not actually held;
- “AI-powered” as proof of superiority.

## Comparative claims

Comparisons should identify the actual baseline and evidence.

Avoid vague “10x better/faster” language without measured conditions.

## Message hierarchy

1. user/problem value;
2. differentiation;
3. proof;
4. mechanism/technology;
5. details/features.

Technology should not obscure the customer outcome.

## Change control

Material company positioning changes require Founder approval and consistency with Company Master.
